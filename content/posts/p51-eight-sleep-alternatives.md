# Eight Sleep Alternatives: Chilipad, BedJet and Cheaper Ways to Sleep Cool

The best Eight Sleep alternatives work in different ways, so price alone won't tell you which is closest. Eight Sleep's Pod pumps temperature-controlled water through a mattress cover. Chilipad does the same without a subscription. BedJet blows conditioned air into your bedding instead. And for some hot sleepers, a cheaper change to the room does the job.

We compared each product's official US product page, checked in November 2026. Prices below are list prices before the holiday sales running at the time. This is a research-based comparison, not a hands-on test.

## What You Get With Eight Sleep

Eight Sleep's Pod 6 includes a cover that fits over your mattress and a hub that heats or cools water. Its product page says it can cool or heat each side of the bed from 55°F to 110°F.

The page listed the Pod 6 at $2,899 before a $200 pre-Black Friday discount. Sleep and health tracking and the vibration alarm need an Autopilot membership. Autopilot costs $199 a year for Standard, $299 for Enhanced (which adds a five-year warranty) or $399 for Elite.

![Eight Sleep Pod 6 cover and hub on a bed, with a note that the mattress is not included](shot-eight-sleep-pod-website "Screenshot: Eight Sleep Pod 6 product page, captured November 2026.")

## 1. Chilipad 2.0: The Closest Match Without a Subscription

Chilipad 2.0 from Sleepme is the nearest mechanical match. It's also a water-powered system, and its product page gives a range of 55°F to 115°F.

The big difference is cost. Sleepme states there are no ongoing subscription fees: you can control the temperature on the unit or through its free app. The one-sleeper version for half of a full bed listed at $1,599. Chilipad 2.0 comes with a two-year manufacturer's warranty. It needs distilled water in its reservoir, so plan for that upkeep.

**Choose Chilipad if** you want water-based cooling without paying for a membership.

![Chilipad 2.0 cooling pad on a bed with its control unit showing 67 degrees](shot-chilipad-website "Screenshot: Chilipad 2.0 product page on Sleepme, captured November 2026.")

## 2. BedJet 3: Air Instead of Water

BedJet 3 works differently. It blows cooled or warmed air into your bedding, which helps wick away sweat and body heat. There's no water to fill or clean. The product page says it works with any mattress and bed frame, including adjustable beds, and comes with a free two-year warranty.

Two caveats from BedJet's own page are worth knowing. It cools the whole bed unless you add its Dual Zone Cloud Sheet. And BedJet says that if your bedroom is regularly over 79°F, the heat will overwhelm its cooling. List price was $699.

**Choose BedJet if** you want fast cooling relief, such as for night sweats, at the lowest price of the three.

![BedJet 3 climate control unit with its remote and phone app](shot-bedjet-website "Screenshot: BedJet 3 product page, captured November 2026.")

## 3. Room and Bedding Changes: Try These First

Before you spend hundreds or thousands of dollars, test the cheap fixes. A fan, a slightly cooler thermostat setting and lighter, breathable bedding may be enough for some people. A passive cooling topper can make the mattress feel less warm, but it can't actively set a temperature.

If these help, you've saved the money. If they don't, you've learned that you need active cooling.

## Questions to Ask Before You Buy

- **Two sleepers?** Check whether each side can hold its own temperature. Eight Sleep does this as standard; the others need a dual setup.
- **Noise:** Where does the unit sit, and how loud is it in a quiet room?
- **Upkeep:** Water systems need refilling and cleaning. Air systems need clear airflow under the covers.
- **Software:** What still works if you cancel a membership, or if the company stops supporting the app?

## FAQs

### What Is the Closest Alternative to Eight Sleep?

Chilipad 2.0 is the closest. Like Eight Sleep, it circulates temperature-controlled water through a pad on your mattress. Unlike Eight Sleep, Sleepme says it has no subscription fees.

### Is BedJet Cheaper Than Eight Sleep?

Yes. BedJet 3 listed at $699, compared with $2,899 for the Eight Sleep Pod 6 plus at least $199 a year for Autopilot. But BedJet blows air into your bedding rather than circulating water, so it works differently.

### Does Chilipad Require a Membership?

No. Sleepme says you can control Chilipad on the unit or through its free app with no ongoing subscription costs.

### Do I Need a Subscription for Eight Sleep?

Eight Sleep's product page notes that sleep and health tracking and the vibration alarm require an Autopilot membership. Autopilot starts at $199 a year.
