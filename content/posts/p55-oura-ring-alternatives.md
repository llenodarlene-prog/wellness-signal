# Oura Ring Alternatives: Smart Rings Without a Monthly Fee

Most people looking at Oura Ring alternatives want the same thing: sleep and recovery tracking without a monthly membership. Oura's ring costs $399 or more, and its detailed insights need a membership. Several rivals now sell rings with no subscription at all. The trade-offs are in battery life, phone compatibility and how polished the app is.

We compared the rings using each maker's official US store and product pages, checked in November 2026. This is a research-based comparison, not a hands-on test, and no brand paid to be included.

## The Benchmark: Oura Ring 5

Oura's 2026 buying guide lists Oura Ring 5 at $399 in silver or black and $499 in other finishes. It weighs as little as 2 grams, and Oura says it's 40% smaller than the previous generation. Battery life is 6 to 9 days, depending on size and features.

The membership is the key cost. It's $5.99 a month or $69.99 a year. Without it, Oura's guide says the app shows only your three daily scores, the ring's battery level and Explore content.

![Oura homepage showing the smart ring on a hand](shot-oura-website "Screenshot: Oura homepage, captured November 2026.")

## 1. RingConn Gen 2 Air: Best Budget Pick

At $199, the Gen 2 Air costs half the price of an Oura Ring 5, and RingConn charges no subscription. Its product page says it's 2 mm thin, weighs 2.5 grams and lasts up to 10 days on a charge. It tracks sleep stages, heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, stress and activity. It also predicts periods from temperature trends.

**Choose the Gen 2 Air if** you want the lowest total cost.

![RingConn Gen 2 Air smart ring showing its inner sensors](shot-ringconn-gen-2-air-product "Image: RingConn Gen 2 Air product image from RingConn, captured November 2026.")

## 2. RingConn Gen 3: Longest Battery Life

The Gen 3 is RingConn's newer flagship at $349, still with no subscription required. Its product page lists battery life of up to 14 days, the longest here. It comes in several finishes. RingConn notes that Gen 3 sizes differ from its other rings and from standard ring sizes, so use the free sizing kit.

**Choose the Gen 3 if** you hate charging and want premium finishes without a membership.

![RingConn Gen 3 smart ring in silver on its product page](shot-ringconn-gen-3-website "Screenshot: RingConn Gen 3 product page, captured November 2026.")

## 3. Samsung Galaxy Ring: For Samsung Phone Owners

The Galaxy Ring starts at $299.99 before trade-in, according to Samsung's US page. It feeds data into the Samsung Health app.

There's a big catch: Samsung's page says you need a Samsung account registered on a Samsung Galaxy smartphone to use it. Battery life is up to 7 days for sizes 12 and 13 and up to 6 days for smaller sizes. Samsung lists an IP68 rating and 10ATM water resistance.

**Choose the Galaxy Ring if** you already use a Galaxy phone.

![Samsung Galaxy Ring in black, silver and gold finishes](shot-samsung-galaxy-ring-website "Screenshot: Samsung Galaxy Ring product page, captured November 2026.")

## 4. Ultrahuman Ring PRO: Longest-Lasting Premium Ring

Ultrahuman's US store lists the Ring PRO at $479. Its product page describes a titanium ring with sleep and recovery tracking and up to 15 days of battery life, or about 12 days in its higher-power Turbo Mode. Ultrahuman says a PRO charging case extends that to up to 45 days.

Smart rings have been the subject of patent disputes in the US, so buy from Ultrahuman's official US store and check the warranty terms. We couldn't confirm from Ultrahuman's page whether any Ring PRO features need a paid subscription, so check that before you buy.

**Choose the Ring PRO if** you want the longest battery life among premium rings.

## What to Check Before You Buy

- **Phone compatibility:** Confirm the ring works with your phone before ordering.
- **Sizing:** Order the free sizing kit. Smart ring sizes don't always match jewelry sizes, and fingers swell during exercise and sleep.
- **Data access:** Can you see your raw trends and export them, or only scores?
- **Warranty and returns:** Check the return window. Oura offers 30 days on its own store.
- **Software support:** A ring depends on its app. Buy from the maker's official US store.

Sleep and readiness scores are estimates. Rings infer sleep stages from heart rate, movement and temperature, not the brain activity a sleep lab measures. Use them to watch trends. ## FAQs

### Is There a Smart Ring Without a Subscription?

Yes. RingConn says its Gen 2 Air ($199) and Gen 3 ($349) need no subscription. Samsung's Galaxy Ring uses the Samsung Health app, and Samsung's page doesn't list a membership fee for it.

### What Is the Cheapest Oura Ring Alternative?

The RingConn Gen 2 Air, at $199 with no subscription. Over three years it costs about $410 less than an Oura Ring 5 with an annual membership.

### Does the Samsung Galaxy Ring Work With iPhone?

No. Samsung's US page says you need a Samsung account registered on a Samsung Galaxy smartphone to use the Galaxy Ring.

### What Does Oura Show Without a Membership?

According to Oura's 2026 guide, the app shows only your three daily scores (Readiness, Sleep and Activity), your ring's battery level and Explore content. Detailed insights need the $5.99 monthly or $69.99 yearly membership.
