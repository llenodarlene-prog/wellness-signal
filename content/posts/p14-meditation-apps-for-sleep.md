# 8 Meditation Apps for Sleep Compared by Free Features and Audio Options

A meditation app for sleep can offer a short guided wind-down, a long bedtime story or a soundtrack that continues after the listener falls asleep. Those experiences sound similar in app descriptions, yet free access, paid libraries and playback controls vary substantially between providers.

This comparison examines eight meditation apps for sleep using information published by their operators. It focuses on practical selection criteria rather than claiming that any app has been independently tested or clinically proven to treat insomnia.

## Key Takeaways

- Medito offers sleep meditation, stories and ambient audio without subscriptions, advertisements or account requirements.
- Insight Timer provides a large free meditation and sleep library, while MemberPlus adds options such as downloads and premium content.
- Calm's help centre (updated October 2026) says its free catalogue includes more than 230 meditations and eight free sleep sessions.
- Headspace offers Sleepcasts, wind-down exercises and Nighttime SOS, but its complete library requires a trial or subscription.
- BetterSleep changed to a premium subscription model in 2025; new users now receive a free trial rather than a permanent full free tier.
- Research on digital mindfulness interventions suggests possible sleep benefits, but the results do not establish that each commercial app treats clinical insomnia.

The selection includes commercial subscription services, nonprofit meditation platforms and meditation libraries with sleep-oriented content. Every entry was assessed using official website or help-centre material available at the research date.

We compared access conditions, sleep-specific formats, audio controls and whether an app requires ongoing payment. The comparison does not assign numerical product scores or claim hands-on performance testing.

## 1. Medito: A Fully Free Sleep Meditation Library

[Medito](https://meditofoundation.org/medito-app/) is a nonprofit-developed meditation platform offering sleep stories, guided sessions, breathing exercises and ambient sounds. Its official website says there are no advertisements, paywalls, premium memberships or account requirements.

That structure makes Medito relevant to listeners who want a bedtime routine without tracking trial periods or renewal dates. It is also a useful benchmark when comparing [free meditation apps](/mind-sleep/free-meditation-apps-compared/) and deciding whether a competitor's free plan provides sufficient ongoing access.

### Who Might Prefer Medito?

Someone who wants to open an app, select a sleep exercise and avoid a recurring charge may find Medito straightforward. The app offers more than one sleep format, which can accommodate preferences for guided instructions, stories or background sounds.

Its nonprofit model is not evidence that each session is clinically superior to paid content. Choice of voice, session length and personal preference remain important even when price is removed from the equation.

## 2. Insight Timer: Free Variety and a Paid Offline Option

[Insight Timer](https://insighttimer.com/) offers a broad catalogue covering mindfulness, guided meditation, sleep recordings and soundscapes. The company's March 2026 support information describes more than 300,000 free guided practices and tracks across its platform.

MemberPlus, listed by the company at $9.99 monthly or $59.99 annually in its 2026 support page, adds premium content and offline downloading. These are published US-oriented prices and should be checked at checkout.

### Free Listening Versus Offline Access

Insight Timer states that its meditation timer works offline for free members, but downloading guided courses is generally a MemberPlus feature. A listener who streams at home may have different needs from someone travelling without reliable connectivity.

For that reason, the free content count should not be the only selection factor. Playback access, search options and a listener's preferred teachers may influence day-to-day usefulness more than library size.

## 3. Calm: Sleep Stories and a Verified Free Selection

[Calm](https://www.calm.com/) combines guided meditation with Sleep Stories, ambient audio and music. Its help centre, updated in October 2026, specifically identifies more than 230 free meditation sessions and eight free sleep sessions, a larger offering than older articles sometimes describe.

Premium expands the available Sleep Stories and broader content catalogue. Calm distinguishes locked material within the application, allowing listeners to identify which sessions require payment.

### When Calm's Free Library May Be Enough

Someone who wants a modest selection of bedtime audio may be able to use Calm without Premium. The free content is not the same as unrestricted access to the full sleep library, and the selection can change.

Calm's production style and story format may be a deciding factor for listeners, but aesthetic preference is subjective. We have not conducted sound-quality tests or compared narrator effectiveness in a controlled study.

## 4. Headspace: Structured Wind-Down and Nighttime SOS

[Headspace](https://www.headspace.com/) provides Sleepcasts, wind-down activities, sleep music, extended sleep radio and Nighttime SOS exercises. Its help centre describes these as separate components intended for bedtime preparation and periods of nighttime waking.

Headspace offers trial access to its full library, with subscription needed for ongoing full-app access afterward. Its free support article points to YouTube, articles and other external resources rather than guaranteeing a permanent broad free sleep library inside the app.

### Who Might Prefer a Structured Sleep Programme?

A listener who appreciates predictable exercises and guided progressions may prefer Headspace's structured presentation. Nighttime SOS also provides a specific format for moments when someone wakes during the night.

These features make Headspace different from a library-first product such as Insight Timer. They do not establish that Headspace provides medical treatment for chronic insomnia, even when its content supports relaxation.

## 5. BetterSleep: SleepTales, Meditation and Custom Audio

[BetterSleep](https://www.bettersleep.com/) focuses strongly on sleep audio, offering guided meditations, SleepTales, soundscapes and a multi-sound mixer. Its library page advertises more than 500 bedtime stories, guided meditations, hypnosis tracks and other audio.

A June 2026 support update clarifies that BetterSleep stopped operating as a freemium product in 2025. New users can trial the app, but an active subscription is needed for continued access to the full library.

### Audio Controls and the Cost of Access

BetterSleep documents playback timers, layering of sounds and offline access for previously downloaded SleepTales and meditations. Its public library page advertised a seven-day trial followed by $59.99 annually when checked for this article.

Those controls may appeal to people who want to create a consistent audio environment. However, readers should assess whether they will regularly use the subscription features rather than relying on the free trial alone.

## 6. Plum Village: Mindfulness and Deep Relaxation

The [Plum Village app](https://plumvillage.app/) offers free guided meditation and deep-relaxation practices associated with Thich Nhat Hanh's monastic community. Its emphasis is broader mindfulness and contemplative practice rather than a commercial library marketed solely around sleep.

Listeners seeking a calmer bedtime routine may find deep-relaxation sessions relevant. Those who prefer cinematic sleep stories, layered ambient tracks or extensive playback customisation may prefer a different app format.

### A Different Editorial Approach to Sleep Audio

Plum Village draws from a recognizable teaching tradition and offers content through a nonprofit-oriented platform. That identity is a content distinction, not a guarantee of better sleep outcomes for every listener.

The app is best assessed on whether its teaching style, voice and session pacing fit a person's preferences. Medical claims should not be inferred simply because a relaxation practice feels beneficial.

## 7. Healthy Minds Program: Structured Mindfulness Skills

[Healthy Minds Program](https://hminnovations.org/meditation-app) offers free science-informed training in mindfulness and wellbeing skills. It is organised around an educational approach rather than primarily a large collection of entertainment-style bedtime stories.

This makes it relevant for someone who wants to develop a regular meditation practice that might extend into bedtime. The programme should not be represented as an insomnia-treatment app without specific evidence for that indication.

### Why Structure Can Be More Useful Than Library Size

A large audio library provides choice, but some users prefer progressive instruction that reduces decisions at the end of the day. Structured meditation can help someone practise attention and relaxation skills consistently.

The important distinction is between a programme's design and its verified clinical outcomes. A scientifically informed curriculum is not identical to a clinical trial proving that its consumer app treats a sleep disorder.

## 8. UCLA Mindful: University-Provided Guided Practice

[UCLA Mindful](https://www.uclahealth.org/programs/marc) provides guided mindfulness practices and educational resources associated with UCLA Health. Its material includes short sessions and body-focused exercises suitable for general mindfulness practice.

This is a useful option for someone wanting simple, free instruction without purchasing a large entertainment catalogue. As with other educational resources, it should not be treated as a substitute for assessment or therapy for ongoing insomnia.

### Where UCLA Mindful Fits in a Bedtime Routine

A brief body scan or breathing practice can provide a manageable exercise before sleep. The choice depends on whether the listener prefers instructor-led practice over stories or continuously playing background sounds.

University affiliation signals the source of the educational material, not superiority over every other app. The relevant question is whether the session is accessible and practical for the listener's routine.

## What Does Research Say About Digital Meditation and Sleep?

Research supports examining mindfulness as a possible contributor to improved sleep, but results vary by intervention and population. It does not follow that every meditation app delivers the same outcomes or qualifies as a treatment for diagnosed insomnia.

A systematic review of standalone digital mindfulness interventions published in *npj Digital Medicine* included 18 randomised controlled trials, with 4,870 participants in the sleep analyses, and rated the evidence as very low certainty. Study designs, clinical populations and measured sleep outcomes differed, so its conclusions should not be used as a product-by-product ranking.

A separate 2025 review of mobile cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia included 16 studies involving 2,146 participants. Mobile CBT-I is a specific intervention, not the same as general guided meditation or bedtime storytelling.

### Meditation Apps Are Not Equivalent to CBT-I

Cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia is a structured treatment for chronic insomnia, whereas meditation apps often provide general relaxation and mindfulness content. Some digital programmes incorporate evidence-based therapeutic components, but they require evaluation on their own terms.

People experiencing persistent insomnia, significant daytime impairment or possible sleep disorders should seek appropriate clinical assessment. A pleasant bedtime meditation may complement a routine but should not delay needed care.

## How to Choose an App for a Sleep Routine

The most useful app is often the one whose content and access rules match a person's actual habits. A brief comparison based on bedtime behaviour can be more practical than choosing the product with the highest advertised track count.

1. **Choose your audio format.** Decide whether you prefer guided breathing, body scans, stories or ambient sound.
2. **Check free access.** Distinguish permanently free content from a trial that converts to payment.
3. **Review nighttime controls.** Consider timers, repeat settings and offline availability.
4. **Check voice and pacing.** Narration style may influence whether a session feels comfortable.
5. **Confirm renewal terms.** Review annual versus monthly billing and the relevant cancellation process.
6. **Consider privacy.** Read the provider's data practices, especially if the app collects sleep or health information or connects to a [sleep-tracking wearable](/longevity-recovery/whoop-vs-oura/).

Apps also differ in whether they require an account and how much device interaction is needed at bedtime. A simple routine may be preferable to extensive browsing if the purpose is to wind down.

## FAQs

### Which Meditation App for Sleep Is Completely Free?

Medito states that its sleep stories, meditations and breathing exercises are free without paywalls or account requirements. Plum Village and other nonprofit resources also provide free mindfulness practices, although their content formats differ from commercial sleep-story libraries.

### Is Calm Free for Sleep Meditation?

Yes, Calm's October 2026 support information lists eight free sleep sessions and more than 230 free meditations. The full Sleep Stories catalogue and other Premium content require a paid subscription, so availability should be checked inside the app.

### Does Headspace Include Sleep Stories Without a Subscription?

Headspace offers full-library access during an eligible trial, then generally requires a subscription for continued full access. Its free help information points to external resources, so readers should not assume that the complete Sleepcast catalogue stays unlocked indefinitely.

### Is BetterSleep a Free App?

BetterSleep's June 2026 support documentation says it transitioned away from a freemium model in 2025. New users may access a free trial, but continued access to the full library requires a subscription under the current published model.

### Can Sleep Meditation Apps Treat Insomnia?

General sleep meditation apps may support relaxation, but they are not interchangeable with evidence-based treatment for chronic insomnia. Research on digital mindfulness and mobile CBT-I evaluates specific programmes, and persistent symptoms warrant qualified clinical assessment.

### Which Sleep Meditation App Works Offline?

Offline access differs by provider and content type. Insight Timer's basic timer works without an internet connection, while downloading guided courses requires MemberPlus; BetterSleep supports downloaded meditations and stories, but its music library requires streaming.

## Choosing Sleep Audio Without Overpaying

For a no-subscription starting point, Medito offers a clear free-access policy, while Insight Timer provides extensive free variety. Calm, Headspace and BetterSleep may suit people who prefer their distinct styles and paid content, provided the subscription is worthwhile.

The right choice depends on sleep-audio preferences, repeat use and access conditions rather than claims that one app is clinically superior. A sleep application can be a useful routine aid without being a substitute for appropriate care when sleep problems persist.
