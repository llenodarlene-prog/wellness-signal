# Chemical Peel Aftercare: Why Recovery Depends on Peel Depth

Chemical peel aftercare depends almost entirely on how deep the peel went. A light "lunchtime" peel may leave mild redness and flaking for a few days. A medium peel can mean swelling and crusting for up to two weeks. A deep peel creates a wound that needs dressings and several follow-up visits. One generic recovery plan can't cover all three.

**Quick answer:** Follow your dermatologist's instructions for your specific peel. Keep your skin moist as directed, don't pick at peeling skin, protect it from the sun and call your dermatologist if your skin burns, itches or swells.

## Chemical Peel Recovery by Depth

The American Academy of Dermatology (AAD) gives these general expectations for peels done by a dermatologist:

| | Light peel | Medium peel | Deep peel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Healing time | 1 to 7 days | 7 to 14 days | 14 to 21 days, bandaged |
| At-home care | Lotion or cream until healed, then daily sunscreen | Daily soaks, ointment after each soak, antiviral medicine for 10 to 14 days | 4 to 6 daily soaks, ointment for 14 days, then thick moisturizer; antiviral for 10 to 14 days |
| Sun | Daily sunscreen after healing | Total sun avoidance until healed | Total sun avoidance for 3 to 6 months |
| Makeup | Usually right away, sometimes the next day | Camouflaging makeup after 5 to 7 days | At least 14 days |
| Follow-up | No | Yes | Next day, then several visits in the first week |

These are typical ranges. Your dermatologist's plan comes first.

## Light Peels

Even with a light peel, freshly treated skin can sting with products you normally use. Don't restart acids, scrubs or retinoids until your dermatologist says it's okay. The AAD notes that some light peels need to be repeated to get the results you want.

## Medium and Deep Peels

These need more than "moisturize and wait." After a medium peel, the skin crusts and peels off over 7 to 14 days, and you may need cool compresses. A deep peel leaves a wound that needs a surgical dressing, and the AAD says it requires 2 to 3 weeks of recovery at home. Both involve antiviral medication and a follow-up visit.

## Five Aftercare Rules From Dermatologists

1. **Don't pick.** DermNet warns that picking delays healing and causes scarring. The AAD says scratching treated skin or picking off a scab raises the risk of infection and scarring.
2. **Keep skin moist.** The AAD says skin that dries and cracks can scar, so use the moisturizer as directed.
3. **Protect it from the sun.** Follow your peel's sun rules, and once healed, use sunscreen daily to keep your results.
4. **Skip tanning beds.** The AAD says indoor tanning stops your skin from healing properly.
5. **Follow your wound care plan.** The AAD says serious side effects often happen when people don't follow their dermatologist's instructions.

## Possible Side Effects

The AAD lists these possible side effects, which tend to be mild in experienced hands:

- Redness that may last for months
- Temporary darkening of the skin
- Lighter skin color, after medium and deep peels
- Scarring, which is very rare when a dermatologist does the peel

DermNet notes that people with darker skin types (Fitzpatrick IV to VI) have a higher risk of pigment changes and raised scarring, so peels should be done cautiously. Choosing an experienced dermatologist matters.

## When to Call Your Dermatologist

The AAD says to contact your dermatologist if your skin burns, itches or swells. Also call promptly about increasing redness or pain, discharge, fever or anything that worries you. DermNet notes that problems are easier to treat early than late. Get an after-hours number before you leave the clinic.

## FAQs

### How Long Does a Chemical Peel Take to Heal?

According to the AAD, a light peel takes 1 to 7 days, a medium peel 7 to 14 days and a deep peel 14 to 21 days.

### When Can I Wear Makeup After a Chemical Peel?

Usually right away or the next day after a light peel, camouflaging makeup after 5 to 7 days for a medium peel, and at least 14 days after a deep peel, per the AAD.

### Should I Peel Off Loose Skin?

No. Picking delays healing and can cause scarring or infection. Let it come off on its own and keep the skin moist as directed.

### Is Redness Normal After a Chemical Peel?

Yes. The AAD notes redness can last for months in some people. Redness that gets worse, especially with pain, swelling, fever or discharge, needs prompt attention.
