# Noom Alternatives: 4 Apps for Coaching, Food Tracking and Habits

The best Noom alternatives depend on which part of Noom you want to keep. Noom bundles daily lessons on habits, food logging, coaching and, for eligible members, access to GLP-1 medications. Most alternatives do one or two of those things well. Start by naming the part you need, then pick the app built for it.

We compared four options using each app's official App Store listing, checked in November 2026. This is a research-based comparison, not a hands-on test, and no app paid to be included.

## Noom Alternatives at a Glance

| App | Best for | Coaching | Free food logging |
|---|---|---|---|
| WeightWatchers | A structured program with community | Live coach-led sessions; 1:1 dietitians | Paid membership |
| Lose It! | A simple calorie budget | None built in | Yes; barcode scanning is Premium |

## What Noom Actually Includes

Noom's App Store listing describes daily lessons on habits and behavior change, AI food logging, meal plans, optional 1:1 coaching and community support. Eligible members can also get GLP-1 medications through Noom's clinicians.

That bundle matters when you compare. A food diary replaces the logging, not the lessons or the coaching. If those are what kept you going, a calorie counter alone may not fill the gap.

## 1. WeightWatchers: The Closest Structured Program

WeightWatchers is the nearest match if you want a full program rather than a tracker. Its App Store listing describes the Points program with ZeroPoint foods, a barcode scanner, and macro tracking for protein, fiber and carbs. It also lists live virtual sessions led by weight loss coaches and 1:1 support from registered dietitians.

Like Noom, WeightWatchers now offers clinical support. Weight Watchers Med+ gives eligible members access to clinicians and weight-loss medication prescriptions.

**Choose WeightWatchers if** you want structure, a community and access to human coaches.

![WeightWatchers App Store listing showing Points, macros and progress tracking](shot-weightwatchers-app-store "Screenshot: WeightWatchers listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

## 2. Lose It!: The Easiest Calorie Budget

Lose It! sets a daily calorie budget from your profile and goal, then lets you log food, weight and exercise. Its free features include calorie tracking, a weight loss plan and community groups.

Its listing puts several handy tools behind Premium: the barcode scanner, photo and voice logging, macro and nutrient tracking, intermittent fasting and meal planning. Lose It! doesn't offer coaching, so it suits people happy to manage their own routine.

**Choose Lose It! if** you want a simple, self-directed calorie budget.

![Lose It! App Store listing showing meal logging and a daily calorie budget](shot-lose-it-app-store "Screenshot: Lose It! listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

## 3. Cronometer: Best for Nutrient Detail

Cronometer goes deeper than calories. Its listing says it tracks calories, macros and more than 95 vitamins and minerals. Its curated database holds more than 1.1 million verified foods.

The free version covers the detailed food diary. Cronometer Gold adds photo and voice logging, a fasting tracker, a recipe importer, an ad-free experience and the Crono Coach feature. That level of detail can feel like a lot if you just want a quick daily log.

**Choose Cronometer if** you care about protein, fiber, vitamins and minerals, not just calories.

![Cronometer App Store listing showing macro and micronutrient tracking](shot-cronometer-app-store "Screenshot: Cronometer listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

## 4. MyNetDiary: The Most Generous Free Tier

MyNetDiary's free version includes more than most. Its listing names barcode scanning, Meal Scan photo logging, macro tracking, 108 nutrients, exercise tracking and progress charts. It's ad-free and needs no account.

It also has a feature called Professional Connect, which lets you share your diary with a dietitian, coach or doctor. That's useful if you want human feedback without paying for a full coaching program. An AI Coach is available on the Premium Plus plan.

**Choose MyNetDiary if** you want a full-featured food diary without paying.

![MyNetDiary App Store listing showing barcode scanning and Meal Scan food logging](shot-mynetdiary-app-store "Screenshot: MyNetDiary listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

## When a Person Beats an App

Apps can organize what you eat, but they can't assess your health. If you have a medical condition, take medications that affect weight or appetite, or have a history of disordered eating, a registered dietitian or your doctor is a better starting point than any app. Detailed calorie logging also isn't right for everyone.

For the bigger picture, the CDC notes that people who lose weight gradually and steadily, at about 1 to 2 pounds a week, are more likely to keep it off. No app changes that.

## A Quick Checklist Before You Switch

- **Coaching:** Is it a named human coach, a group session or automated messages?
- **Food database:** Does it cover the meals and brands you actually eat?
- **Free limits:** Is barcode scanning or nutrient tracking behind a paywall?
- **Your data:** Can you export your history if you leave?
- **Total cost:** Compare yearly prices, including any coaching add-ons, not just intro offers.

## FAQs

### What Is the Best Free Alternative to Noom?

MyNetDiary has one of the most generous free tiers, with barcode scanning, photo logging and 108 nutrients. Cronometer is free for detailed nutrient tracking. Neither includes Noom's daily lessons or human coaching, so free means logging, not a full program.

### Is WeightWatchers Better Than Noom?

They're similar in scope. Both offer structured programs, community and clinical support with weight-loss medications for eligible members. WeightWatchers uses its Points system and live coach-led sessions, while Noom centers on daily psychology-based lessons. The better one is the program you'll actually stick with.

### Which App Tracks Vitamins and Minerals?

Cronometer tracks more than 95 vitamins and minerals, and MyNetDiary tracks 108 nutrients on its free plan. Lose It! puts detailed nutrient tracking behind its Premium plan.

### Can an App Replace a Dietitian?

No. Apps can help you record food and build habits, but they can't assess your health, medications or medical history. MyNetDiary's Professional Connect and WeightWatchers' dietitian support can link you to a professional.
