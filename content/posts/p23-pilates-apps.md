# 8 Pilates Apps Compared for Mat, Reformer and Beginner Workouts

Pilates apps cover a surprisingly wide range. Pilatesology is built around classical Pilates. Pilates Anytime has a deep library of mat, reformer and apparatus work. Lindywell focuses on short mat sessions and balanced living, while FitOn and ALO Wellness Club tuck Pilates into much bigger fitness libraries.

That changes what good value looks like. Someone with a reformer at home needs a different library from a beginner who wants 15-minute mat sessions with no equipment.

We compared eight apps by method, equipment, beginner structure, library focus, free access and price. We checked US App Store listings and official product pages in early November 2026. We didn't take the classes ourselves, so we don't rate instructors or claim one program gets better results.

For background on the method, see [Pilates vs yoga](/fitness/pilates-vs-yoga/) and [Pilates vs strength training](/fitness/pilates-vs-strength-training/). If studio prices are part of your decision, try our [Pilates Class Cost Calculator](/pilates-class-cost-calculator/).

## 1. Pilates Anytime: Best for a Deep Specialist Library

![Pilates Anytime App Store artwork of an instructor stretching on a mat](shot-pilates-anytime-app-store "Screenshot: Pilates Anytime listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

Pilates Anytime is one of the most Pilates-focused services here. Its library spans mat, reformer and other apparatus work. There are classes for students plus teaching material that instructors may find useful.

The US App Store lists a **14-day free trial**, then **$24.99 a month or $264.99 a year**.

### Who It Suits

The price makes most sense if Pilates is your main training, especially with a reformer or other equipment at home. If you only want one or two mat sessions a week, cheaper options exist.

**Best fit:** Serious home practice, reformer owners and anyone who values a specialist library.

## 2. Pilatesology: Best for Classical Pilates

![Pilatesology app menu with beginner quick start and classical workout sections](shot-pilatesology-app-store "Screenshot: Pilatesology listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

Pilatesology positions itself around classical Pilates rather than a fitness hybrid. The library includes mat and reformer work, structured progressions and material for students and teachers.

You can download and browse for free, but the full library needs a membership. The US App Store lists **$22.99 a month or $194.99 a year**.

### Who It Suits

It's strongest for people who want the classical method specifically, without paying for a yoga or meditation bundle on top.

**Best fit:** Classical Pilates students, teachers and reformer users.

## 3. Glo: Best for Pilates Mixed With Yoga and Meditation

![Glo App Store artwork of a woman in a yoga stretch under the words Feel good inside and out](shot-glo-app-store "Screenshot: Glo listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

Glo puts Pilates next to a large yoga, meditation and fitness library. You'll find it alongside barre, conditioning, breathwork and several yoga styles.

The listing offers a **7-day free trial**. Standard App Store pricing is **$30 a month or $244.99 a year**.

### Who It Suits

Glo is easier to justify when Pilates is one part of a wider movement routine. If you only want specialist reformer teaching, Pilates Anytime or Pilatesology is a closer fit.

**Best fit:** People who split their time between Pilates, yoga and mindfulness.

## 4. Lindywell: Best for Short Mat Sessions and Habit Building

![Lindywell App Store artwork of a woman doing mat Pilates on a beach, 500 plus on-demand workouts](shot-lindywell-app-store "Screenshot: Lindywell listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

Lindywell is built around mat Pilates and balanced-living content. Many workouts take about 15 minutes. It combines movement with recipes, breathing exercises and other wellness material.

Access comes with a Lindywell membership. The US App Store currently lists **$37 a month or $199 a year**.

### Who It Suits

It's a practical choice if consistency and short home workouts matter more to you than a big reformer catalog.

**Best fit:** Busy beginners and regular mat Pilates users who like short sessions.

## 5. The Pilates Class: Best for Contemporary Pilates and Fusion

![The Pilates Class app screen with an instructor standing in a bright studio](shot-the-pilates-class-app-store "Screenshot: The Pilates Class listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

The Pilates Class blends traditional Pilates ideas with dance, yoga, barre, strength, HIIT and other formats. Its library includes reformer Pilates, pre- and postnatal classes, stretching and breathwork.

The App Store lists **$29 a month, $59 a quarter or $139 a year**.

### Who It Suits

Its annual price is lower than several specialist rivals. That makes it attractive if you enjoy variety and don't need a strictly classical approach.

**Best fit:** Fans of Pilates-inspired fusion who want both mat and reformer options.

## 6. FitOn: Best Free Option for Casual Mat Pilates

![FitOn App Store artwork of a smiling trainer headlined Get in your best shape](shot-fiton-app-store "Screenshot: FitOn listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

FitOn isn't a Pilates specialist. It's a broad fitness platform with Pilates, yoga, cardio, strength, HIIT and meditation.

The key point is access. FitOn says its workout library can be used for free without a subscription. Paid options add extras rather than gating the classes.

### Who It Suits

It's a sensible place to try Pilates at home before paying for a specialist service.

**Best fit:** Budget-conscious beginners who also want other workout styles.

## 7. ALO Wellness Club: Best Free Broad Wellness Library

![ALO Wellness Club App Store artwork of a woman exercising with a weighted ball](shot-alo-wellness-club-app-store "Screenshot: ALO Wellness Club listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

The service once called Alo Moves is now ALO Wellness Club. Its 2026 App Store listing says you can create an account and use the on-demand library without a subscription or credit card. Some specialist programs are sold separately as one-off purchases.

The catalog includes Pilates, yoga, mindfulness, sleep, nutrition and other wellness classes.

### Why the Change Matters

Older comparisons may still describe Alo Moves as a standard paid subscription. That's no longer how the US listing presents it.

**Best fit:** People who want Pilates as part of a broad, free wellness library.

## 8. BetterMe: Best for Pilates Inside a Wider Habit Plan

![BetterMe app screen with Pilates, calisthenics and chair yoga workout tiles](shot-betterme-app-store "Screenshot: BetterMe listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

BetterMe mixes Pilates with strength, yoga, walking, calisthenics, meal logging and other habit tools. It's closer to an all-in-one wellness plan than a Pilates teaching library.

The App Store shows many purchase options and promotions rather than one standard price. Check the plan length and renewal price carefully at checkout.

### Who It Suits

It suits someone who wants Pilates folded into a bigger weight or habit plan. It's less suited to detailed classical or reformer instruction.

**Best fit:** People who want Pilates inside a broader wellness program.

## FAQs

### What Is the Best Free Pilates App?

FitOn and ALO Wellness Club are the clearest free starting points here. FitOn includes Pilates in a broad fitness library, while ALO Wellness Club offers a wider wellness catalog without a subscription. Neither is as Pilates-focused as Pilates Anytime or Pilatesology.

### Which Pilates App Has Reformer Workouts?

Pilates Anytime, Pilatesology and The Pilates Class all include reformer content. Pilatesology leans classical, while The Pilates Class mixes reformer work with contemporary formats.

### Which Pilates App Is Best for Beginners?

FitOn is a no-cost way to try mat Pilates. Lindywell focuses on short sessions and routine, while Pilatesology offers structured classical teaching. Choose based on whether you want general fitness, quick mat sessions or method-specific learning.

### Can a Pilates App Replace Studio Classes?

Not entirely. An app makes instruction easier to access, but it can't correct your form or adjust equipment in real time. If you're new to the reformer, returning after injury or dealing with pain, in-person classes are worth considering first.

### How Much Do Pilates Apps Cost?

The specialist subscriptions here run from about $23 to $37 a month at standard monthly prices. FitOn and ALO Wellness Club offer substantial free access. Annual billing lowers the monthly cost, but value depends on how often you'll use it.
