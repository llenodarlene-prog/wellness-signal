# Dermaplaning vs Microdermabrasion: Two Ways to Exfoliate, Different Tradeoffs

Dermaplaning vs microdermabrasion is a choice between two kinds of surface exfoliation. Dermaplaning uses a small blade to scrape away dead skin cells and fine facial hair. Microdermabrasion uses tiny crystals or a diamond-tipped wand, plus suction, to buff away the top layer of skin. Both aim for smoother-looking skin, but they work differently and suit different goals.

**Quick answer:** Choose dermaplaning if you also want to remove peach fuzz. Choose microdermabrasion for gentle resurfacing with no downtime. Neither reliably fixes deep acne scars.

## Dermaplaning vs Microdermabrasion at a Glance

| | Dermaplaning | Microdermabrasion |
|---|---|---|
| Tool | A scalpel-like blade or razor | Crystals sprayed through a tube, or a diamond-tipped wand, with suction |
| Removes peach fuzz | Yes, temporarily | Not its purpose |
| Exfoliates dead skin | Yes | Yes |
| Downtime | Usually little; some redness or swelling | None, per the AAD |
| Deep acne scars | May not help | Not a treatment for deep scars |
| Main risks | Nicks, irritation, infection, skin color changes | Irritation; dark spots in people prone to them |

## How Dermaplaning Works

A provider holds a sterile blade at an angle and gently scrapes the skin's surface, removing dead skin cells and the soft, light facial hair called peach fuzz. Cleveland Clinic notes that hair removal is temporary because the hair grows back. Afterward, a provider may apply a soothing gel or ointment for redness, swelling or irritation.

Cleveland Clinic lists active acne and cold sore outbreaks among the reasons to postpone dermaplaning, and notes it may not reduce the appearance of deep or raised acne scars. Possible side effects include skin turning lighter or darker than the surrounding skin. Contact your provider if swelling lasts longer than a week or gets worse.

## How Microdermabrasion Works

Cleveland Clinic describes two main methods:

- **Crystal microdermabrasion:** tiny crystals are sprayed through a tube to loosen dead skin cells, then vacuumed away with the debris.
- **Diamond microdermabrasion:** a diamond-tipped wand removes and vacuums the top layer of skin.

The American Academy of Dermatology (AAD) says there's no downtime or recovery period, and that when a dermatologist performs it, microdermabrasion has an excellent safety record. For signs of aging, a dermatologist may treat weekly, every two weeks or monthly. Cleveland Clinic notes your skin may be more sensitive to sunlight afterward, so wear sunscreen.

Microdermabrasion isn't the same as a HydraFacial, which adds fluids and serums. Our [HydraFacial vs microdermabrasion](/beauty-aesthetics/hydrafacial-vs-microdermabrasion/) comparison covers that difference.

## Who Should See a Dermatologist First

The AAD says to see a dermatologist before getting microdermabrasion at a spa or using an at-home kit if you:

- Are taking, or have taken, the acne medicine isotretinoin
- Tend to get dark spots after acne clears or a skin injury heals, which may raise your risk of more dark spots

For either treatment, tell your provider about active acne, rashes, cold sores, recent sunburn, prescription retinoids or exfoliating acids. People with darker skin tones should ask specifically about the risk of skin color changes.

## Can Either Fix Acne Scars?

Not deep ones. Both work on the surface of the skin. Cleveland Clinic says dermaplaning may not reduce deep or raised acne scars, and it lists mild scars, especially from acne, among the things microdermabrasion may help. For deeper scarring, a dermatologist can match the treatment to the scar type. Our comparison of [microneedling vs chemical peel](/beauty-aesthetics/microneedling-vs-chemical-peel/) covers two deeper options.

When judging before-and-after photos, remember that lighting, angle and makeup can make surface changes look dramatic.

## FAQs

### Is Dermaplaning or Microdermabrasion Better?

It depends on your goal. Dermaplaning also removes peach fuzz. Microdermabrasion gently resurfaces with no downtime, according to the AAD. Neither is designed to fix deep acne scars.

### Does Dermaplaning Hair Removal Last?

No. Cleveland Clinic notes it's only temporary because the hair grows back.

### Is Microdermabrasion Safe?

The AAD says that when a dermatologist performs it, microdermabrasion has an excellent safety record. See a dermatologist first if you've taken isotretinoin or tend to get dark spots after acne or injuries.

### Can I Have Both in One Appointment?

Combining exfoliating treatments can increase irritation. Whether it's appropriate depends on your skin and the intensity of each treatment, so ask your provider.
