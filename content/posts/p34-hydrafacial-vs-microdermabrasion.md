# HydraFacial vs Microdermabrasion: 7 Differences in Method, Downtime and Cost

HydraFacial and microdermabrasion are both non-surgical facials, but the devices do different things. Traditional microdermabrasion uses mechanical exfoliation plus suction. HydraFacial is a branded system that combines exfoliation, suction and serums applied during the treatment.

There's a catch in the HydraFacial vs microdermabrasion comparison. One is a brand, and the other is a general type of treatment. Here's what each one does and what the evidence supports.

## HydraFacial vs Microdermabrasion at a Glance

| Factor | HydraFacial | Microdermabrasion |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | Exfoliation, suction and serum delivery | Mechanical exfoliation with suction |
| What it is | A branded hydradermabrasion system | A general procedure type |
| Serum step | Built into the treatment | Not part of the standard procedure |
| Exfoliating tool | Wand tips with fluid | Crystals or a diamond-tipped wand |
| Downtime | Usually minimal | Usually minimal |
| Research | Small | Longer history, mixed outcome data |
| Price | Often marketed as premium | Often lower, varies by market |

## 1. HydraFacial Is a Brand

HydraFacial describes its treatment as combining exfoliation, hydration and targeted serums in one session. The general technique is often called hydradermabrasion.

That matters because research on one device or protocol doesn't automatically apply to every "hydro facial" machine on the market.

![HydraFacial Syndeo treatment device with a touchscreen, a blue-lit bank of serum bottles and a wand on a hose](shot-hydrafacial-product-image "Image: HydraFacial official website, November 2026.")

## 2. Microdermabrasion Is Mechanical Resurfacing

Microdermabrasion removes part of the skin's outermost layer through controlled abrasion. Some systems spray fine crystals, while others use a diamond-tipped wand. Suction lifts away the loosened material.

The aim is surface resurfacing, not deeper changes in the skin.

## 3. The Research Is Thinner Than the Marketing

A 2008 randomized study of 20 women aged 34 to 56 compared six hydradermabrasion treatments with an antioxidant serum against applying the same serum by hand. The hydradermabrasion group showed thicker skin layers, higher antioxidant levels and fewer visible fine lines, smaller pores and less pigmentation.

That's useful, but it was small, it's old, and it didn't compare HydraFacial with modern microdermabrasion. A 2024 imaging study added more detail about how hydradermabrasion affects skin. The fair conclusion is that hydradermabrasion has some clinical evidence. It hasn't been shown to beat microdermabrasion.

## 4. Neither Label Guarantees It Suits Sensitive Skin

HydraFacial is often marketed as gentler, because it skips crystal abrasion and adds hydration. Microdermabrasion can be adjusted too, but mechanical exfoliation can irritate some people.

Active eczema, inflamed acne, a rosacea flare or a damaged skin barrier can change what's appropriate. A cosmetic facial shouldn't be used to push through active skin disease.

## 5. Both Usually Have Minimal Downtime

Both are sold as low-downtime treatments. Temporary redness, dryness or sensitivity can still happen. Add-on acids, aggressive passes or combining treatments can make irritation worse than a basic comparison suggests. Ask what's included in the treatment you're booking.

## 6. HydraFacial Leans Into Pore Clearing

HydraFacial's suction and extraction step is a big selling point for clogged pores. Microdermabrasion also clears surface debris, but extraction isn't its main feature.

Neither treats every type of acne. Blackheads, inflamed acne and acne scars are different problems. For persistent acne, a medical treatment plan does more than repeated facials. ## 7. HydraFacial Usually Costs More

HydraFacial is usually sold as a premium branded service, while microdermabrasion is a general treatment. That often makes HydraFacial more expensive, though local prices vary widely.

Compare the full cost:

- the base treatment and any boosters
- LED or other add-ons
- extraction time and treatment area
- package requirements and tipping norms
- how often the provider recommends coming back

A cheaper session can cost more over a year if you're told to come back much more often.

## Which Should You Choose?

Choose based on what you want to change.

## FAQs

### Is HydraFacial the Same as Microdermabrasion?

No. HydraFacial is a branded hydradermabrasion system that combines exfoliation, suction and serum delivery. Traditional microdermabrasion relies mainly on mechanical exfoliation and suction. Both resurface the top layer of skin, but they don't use the same steps.

### Does HydraFacial Have Better Research Than Microdermabrasion?

Not clearly. Hydradermabrasion has some published research, including a small 2008 randomized study, but the evidence is limited. Modern head-to-head trials against microdermabrasion are scarce. A big marketing presence isn't the same as stronger evidence.

### Can HydraFacial or Microdermabrasion Treat Acne Scars?

Light resurfacing may smooth texture for a while, but established acne scars usually need a different approach. Microneedling, peels, lasers and other procedures are more relevant, depending on the scar type. A dermatologist can tell active acne from scarring.

### How Often Should I Get Either Treatment?

There's no evidence-based schedule that fits everyone. How often depends on the treatment strength, how your skin responds, other products or procedures you use and why you're having it. Be wary of packages sold before you know how your skin reacts.
