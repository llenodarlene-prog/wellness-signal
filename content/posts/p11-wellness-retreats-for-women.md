# 9 Wellness Retreats for Women in the US: Programmes, Inclusions and Costs

A women's wellness retreat can mean a structured fitness stay, a yoga weekend, a discussion-led women's health programme, or a quiet gathering in nature. Those experiences have different schedules, participation requirements, and price structures, despite appearing under the same wellness travel label.

This comparison of wellness retreats for women examines nine **specific women's-focused programmes in the United States**, using organisers' published programme descriptions. Some take place in late 2026; others have dates announced for 2027. A listed event is not a confirmation that a space remains available.

## Key Takeaways

- The selected programmes range from resort-based women's health retreats to smaller yoga, creative, and community-focused gatherings.
- **Golden Door** publishes women-only weeks; **Canyon Ranch** offers specialist women's health events alongside its wider resort programming.
- Published examples range from **$495 tuition plus accommodation** for a Kripalu programme to **$1,950 per person per night before taxes** for a Canyon Ranch retreat.
- **Seven Springs** lists an April 2027 women's retreat starting at **$995**, described by the organiser as inclusive of the specified lodging, meals, and programming.
- A retreat described as *all-inclusive* may still exclude flights, transport, taxes, optional treatments, or certain activities.
- Programme descriptions and reported guest experiences cannot establish medical effectiveness or guaranteed long-term health outcomes.

## How We Selected These Retreats

We required an identifiable US venue or organiser, a published women's-specific programme, and enough official detail to describe its focus. Preference went to pages listing an upcoming date, explicit activities, inclusions, or a published price rather than broad claims about relaxation or transformation.

The list is not a ranking of medical effectiveness or hospitality quality. No stay was independently tested, and some events have narrow eligibility, small groups, or fixed dates. [Official programme links](https://www.canyonranch.com/wellness-programs/womens-wellness-retreats) are provided within the relevant entries, not the comparison table.

## 1. Golden Door Women's Week, California

The operator describes a blend of fitness, spa experiences, and mindfulness across its women's weeks. Upcoming 2026 options include the November 15–21 Art of Presence women's week; 2027 dates had not been published when this article was checked.

This is best suited to readers seeking an immersive resort format with a clearly designated women's programme. Prospective guests should request a current itemised quote and confirm the included services, activity modifications, and cancellation conditions before booking.

Those totals do **not** measure the women-only retreat market and cannot be used to infer how many women book organised retreats. They provide broader tourism context only, while the nine offerings above illustrate different programme structures rather than a representative market sample.

## How to Compare Retreat Packages Before Booking

The lowest quoted price is not necessarily the lowest total cost. A retreat charging tuition separately from lodging may ultimately cost more than a package that includes accommodation and meals, depending on the selected room and length of stay.

A practical comparison should cover the same cost categories for every programme:

1. **Dates and availability:** Is the programme scheduled, waitlisted, or already completed?
2. **Accommodation:** Is lodging included, mandatory, shared, or priced separately?
3. **Meals and activities:** Which sessions and food services are actually covered?
4. **Staff and qualifications:** Who leads specialist health, fitness, or counselling sessions?
5. **Accessibility:** Are alternative activity levels, dietary accommodations, and accessible rooms available?
6. **Final cost:** What are the taxes, service charges, transport expenses, and cancellation conditions?

## FAQs

### Are Women's Wellness Retreats Always Women-Only?

No. Some retreats designate women-only dates or explicitly restrict participation, while others market programming toward women's health without excluding other guests from the wider resort. Check the exact event eligibility and accommodation rules rather than relying only on the words “women's retreat” in promotional material.

### How Much Do Wellness Retreats for Women Cost in the US?

Published prices vary by format and inclusions. Seven Springs advertises a four-day programme from $995, while Kripalu lists a $495 tuition fee before mandatory lodging. Canyon Ranch quotes a substantially higher per-person, per-night rate for its specialist Lenox retreat, excluding taxes.

### What Is Usually Included in an All-Inclusive Women's Retreat?

Depending on the operator, a package may include the accommodation, meals, group sessions, selected activities, and some treatments. Flights, airport transfers, premium spa services, taxes, tips, and insurance may be excluded. Compare each provider's written inclusions and exclusions before treating prices as equivalent.

### Are Women's Wellness Retreats Medically Proven to Improve Health?

A retreat may offer rest, movement, and a supportive environment, but its commercial description does not establish clinical effectiveness. Benefits depend on the activities, the participant, and the outcome studied. Retreats offering screenings or specialist health sessions should identify appropriate clinical oversight and qualifications.

### Can Someone Attend a Women's Wellness Retreat Alone?

Some programmes explicitly welcome solo guests, while others accommodate shared rooms or group bookings. Ask about room type, scheduled group activities, accessibility, and guest eligibility before reserving. A smaller women-specific programme may facilitate connection, but the experience will still depend on personal preferences.

### What Is the Difference Between a Women's Health Retreat and a Spa Weekend?

A women's health retreat may focus on structured education, peer discussion, or a particular life stage, sometimes with qualified health professionals. A spa weekend typically emphasises accommodation and personal treatments. There can be overlap, so the actual itinerary and provider credentials are more informative than the label.

## Final Assessment

Women's wellness retreats in the US range from specialist health education to creative workshops and nature-focused yoga gatherings. The useful comparison is between the experiences, guest eligibility, documented inclusions, and full price, rather than a single ranking of the “best” retreat.

Choose the programme that suits your goals and available time, then verify its conditions directly with the organiser. Programme dates, pricing, and even the identity of facilitators can change after an article is published.
