# WHOOP Alternatives: Screen-Free Bands and Rings Without a Membership

The best WHOOP alternatives now include screen-free bands that don't need a membership. WHOOP pioneered the idea of a band with no screen that tracks sleep, strain and recovery, but you pay for it every year. In 2026, Garmin and Polar both launched screen-free bands you buy once. A smart ring is another option if you'd rather keep your wrist free.

We compared the options using each company's official pricing pages and announcements, checked in November 2026. This is a research-based comparison, not a hands-on test, and no brand paid to be included.

## What You Pay for With WHOOP

WHOOP is a membership first. Its US plans are One at $199 a year, Peak at $239 and Life at $359. One and Peak include WHOOP 5.0 hardware, while Life comes with WHOOP MG for added heart-related features. The device comes with the membership, so you keep paying to keep using it.

![WHOOP App Store listing showing recovery, strain and sleep scores](shot-whoop-app-store "Screenshot: WHOOP listing on the Apple App Store, retrieved November 2026.")

## 1. Garmin CIRQA: The Closest Match Without a Fee

Garmin announced CIRQA on July 21, 2026, as its first screen-free smart band. Garmin's press release says it needs no subscription and lasts up to 10 days on a charge. It tracks wrist-based heart rate, Body Battery energy levels, Pulse Ox, stress, skin temperature and sleep.

With no screen, it has no built-in GPS. It borrows your iPhone or Android phone's GPS for outdoor walks, rides and runs. The suggested retail price is $199.99.

**Choose CIRQA if** you want the WHOOP experience and already like Garmin Connect.

![Garmin CIRQA smart bands in gray, pink, blue and black fabric](shot-garmin-cirqa-press-image "Image: Garmin CIRQA press announcement, captured November 2026.")

## 2. Polar Loop: Subscription-Free With Strong Sleep Focus

Polar describes the Loop as a screen-free, subscription-free band for sleep, recovery and daily activity. Its product page lists up to 8 days of battery and 4 weeks of onboard memory, so it keeps recording even if you don't sync for a while. You can start a workout in the Polar Flow app and use your phone's GPS to track your route. List price was $199.99.

**Choose Polar Loop if** you want a one-time purchase from a long-standing heart rate brand.

![Polar Loop screen-free band in Night Black on its product page](shot-polar-loop-website "Screenshot: Polar Loop product page, captured November 2026.")

## 3. Oura Ring 5: Move It Off Your Wrist

If a band bothers you in bed, a ring tracks many of the same signals from your finger. Oura's 2026 buying guide lists Ring 5 from $399, plus a membership of $5.99 a month or $69.99 a year for detailed insights. Battery life is 6 to 9 days.

For subscription-free rings, see our full guide to [Oura Ring alternatives](/longevity-recovery/oura-ring-alternatives/).

**Choose Oura if** you want ring-based tracking and don't mind a smaller membership fee.

![Oura homepage showing the smart ring on a hand](shot-oura-website "Screenshot: Oura homepage, captured November 2026.")

## What About a Garmin Watch?

A Garmin sports watch is a different kind of replacement. It adds a screen, built-in GPS on many models, pace and lap data, and maps. That's great for runners and cyclists, but it's bigger and more distracting than a band. Our [WHOOP vs Garmin](/longevity-recovery/whoop-vs-garmin/) comparison covers that choice in depth.

## Recovery Scores Are Estimates

Every brand blends sleep, heart rate variability and recent activity into its own score. Two devices can give the same person different numbers on the same morning because their formulas differ. Garmin's press release notes CIRQA is not a medical device. Use any of these to spot trends, not to diagnose anything. ## FAQs

### Is There a WHOOP Alternative Without a Subscription?

Yes. Garmin CIRQA and Polar Loop are both screen-free bands that need no subscription, according to Garmin and Polar. Each lists at $199.99.

### Is Garmin CIRQA Better Than WHOOP?

It depends on what you want. CIRQA costs $199.99 once with no subscription and lasts up to 10 days. WHOOP One costs $199 every year but includes WHOOP's coaching-style app. CIRQA uses your phone's GPS for outdoor workouts.

### How Much Does WHOOP Cost per Year?

WHOOP's US plans are One at $199, Peak at $239 and Life at $359 per year. The device is included with the membership.

### Can a Smart Ring Replace WHOOP?

For sleep and recovery tracking, often yes. Rings like Oura Ring 5 track heart rate, sleep and temperature from your finger. They're less suited to tracking workouts than a band or watch.
