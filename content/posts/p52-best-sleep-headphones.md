# Best Sleep Headphones for Side Sleepers: 4 Earbuds and Headbands Compared

The best sleep headphones are the ones you forget you're wearing. Earbuds that sound great on a commute can feel like a pebble after two hours pressed into a pillow. So for sleep, size and shape matter more than sound quality. After fit, the big choice is how they handle noise: active noise cancellation (ANC), which electronically reduces sound, or masking, which covers it with soothing audio.

We compared four options using each maker's official product pages and support documents, checked in November 2026. This is a research-based comparison, not a hands-on test.

## 1. Ozlo Sleepbuds 2: Smallest Earbuds for Side Sleeping

Ozlo launched Sleepbuds 2 in July 2026 and calls them the world's smallest earbuds designed for sleep. Ozlo's product page lists each earbud at 1.6 grams and 11 mm tall, with up to 14 hours of battery life.

They don't have ANC. Ozlo says they use noise masking to reduce disruptions such as snoring and city noise. They can also detect when you fall asleep and switch from streaming to masking sounds. They come with a one-year warranty and a 30-day money-back guarantee.

**Choose Ozlo if** a low profile for side sleeping matters most and masking is enough for your noise.

## 2. soundcore Sleep A30 Special: Best for Active Noise Cancelling

The Sleep A30 Special combines ANC, passive isolation and snore masking guided by a microphone in the case. soundcore pitches it at people who need more than masking, such as for traffic or a partner's snoring. It also tracks sleep duration, position and snoring, and the Special edition includes selected Calm Sleep Stories.

Battery life depends heavily on settings. soundcore's FAQ gives 10 hours by default, but only 7 hours with Bluetooth audio and ANC on all night. Local audio with ANC off reaches 16 hours. Always compare battery figures at the same settings. List price was $199.99.

**Choose the A30 Special if** you need ANC for low, constant noise.

![soundcore Sleep A30 Special earbuds and charging case in lilac](shot-soundcore-sleep-a30-website "Screenshot: soundcore Sleep A30 Special product page, captured November 2026.")

## 3. soundcore Sleep A20: Simpler Earbuds Without ANC

The Sleep A20 is soundcore's lower-priced sleep earbud, at a $179.99 list price. It skips ANC. Instead, it uses twin-seal ear tips for passive blocking plus noise masking.

soundcore lists 14 hours in sleep mode (80 hours with the case) and 10 hours streaming over Bluetooth. A motion sensor tracks your sleeping position, movement and duration in the soundcore app.

**Choose the A20 if** you want sleep earbuds and don't need ANC.

![soundcore Sleep A20 earbuds in a light beige finish](shot-soundcore-sleep-a20-website "Screenshot: soundcore Sleep A20 product page, captured November 2026.")

## 4. SleepPhones Classic: A Soft Headband Instead of Earbuds

If anything in your ear bothers you, a headband moves the speakers out of the ear canal. SleepPhones Classic puts thin speakers inside a soft, washable fabric headband, and the company says it's comfortable to lie on your side. The Classic is corded, so there's no battery to charge. Its list price was $49.95.

Headbands have trade-offs. They don't block much outside noise. The speakers can shift during the night, and some fabrics feel warm.

**Choose a headband if** earbuds are uncomfortable and you want gentle audio, not isolation.

## ANC vs Masking: Which Do You Need?

- **ANC** generates sound designed to cancel steady low-frequency noise, such as traffic or an air conditioner hum.
- **Masking** plays sounds that make disruptions less noticeable. It works well for irregular sounds, but it adds sound rather than removing it.

Neither guarantees silence. If you need to hear a baby monitor, a doorbell or a smoke alarm, test that you can still hear it in your sleeping position before you rely on any device. Keep the volume at the lowest comfortable level.

## How to Test Them at Home

Use the return window to check:

1. **Pressure:** Are there sore spots on your ear after a night on your side?
2. **Alarms:** Can you still hear your alarm and important household sounds?
3. **Battery:** Do they last your full night at the settings you use?
4. **Fit:** Do they stay in or stay in place when you roll over?
5. **Cleaning:** Can you wash the headband or clean the ear tips easily?

## FAQs

### What Are the Best Sleep Headphones for Side Sleepers?

Look for the lowest profile. Ozlo Sleepbuds 2 are among the smallest, at 1.6 grams and 11 mm per earbud according to Ozlo. If earbuds hurt, a soft headband like SleepPhones keeps the speakers outside your ear.

### Do Ozlo Sleepbuds 2 Have Noise Cancelling?

No. Ozlo says Sleepbuds 2 use noise masking technology, not active noise cancellation.

### Is It Safe to Sleep With Earbuds Every Night?

Keep the volume low, clean the ear tips regularly and stop if your ears get sore or irritated. Make sure you can still hear alarms you rely on. If you have ear problems, ask a doctor first.

### Can Sleep Earbuds Block Snoring?

They can reduce it, but not always completely. ANC earbuds like the soundcore Sleep A30 Special target steady low sounds and add snore masking. Masking-only earbuds cover snoring with other sounds rather than cancelling it.
