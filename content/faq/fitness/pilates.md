Pilates is a system of controlled exercises focused on core strength, posture, breathing and precise movement. It has become one of the fastest-growing ways Americans work out. Among US gym and studio members, Pilates participation grew 28.1% between 2021 and 2025, according to Health & Fitness Association data in our [fitness statistics](/fitness/fitness-statistics-by-activity-and-age/).

These answers cover what Pilates is, the difference between mat and reformer classes, what the research shows and what it costs. Where we've covered a question in depth, we link to the full article.

## Pilates Basics

What Pilates is and how classes differ.

### What is Pilates?

Pilates is an exercise method built around control, alignment, breathing and trunk stability. Moves are usually slow and precise rather than fast or high-impact. You can do it on a mat using your body weight, or on equipment such as a reformer, which uses springs for resistance.

### What's the difference between mat and reformer Pilates?

Mat Pilates uses your body weight and gravity. Reformer Pilates adds a sliding carriage, springs and straps. ACE Fitness notes that the springs can either add resistance or help you through a move, so the reformer isn't automatically harder. Our [reformer Pilates vs mat Pilates](/fitness/reformer-pilates-vs-mat-pilates/) comparison covers the differences, costs and research in detail.

### Is Pilates good for beginners?

Yes, if the class is pitched at the right level. Mat and reformer classes can both work for beginners. A skilled instructor who can adjust exercises for you matters more than the equipment. Beginner-specific classes help you learn the breathing, setup and basic moves safely.

## Benefits and Research

What Pilates can and can't do.

### Does Pilates build muscle?

Pilates builds strength and muscular endurance, especially in your core, and reformer springs add adjustable resistance. It isn't a direct substitute for heavy strength training if your goal is maximum strength or muscle size. Our [Pilates vs strength training](/fitness/pilates-vs-strength-training/) comparison explains how the two fit together.

### Is Pilates good for back pain?

It may help, but the evidence is limited. A 2015 Cochrane review of 10 trials with 510 people found low- to moderate-quality evidence that Pilates reduced pain and disability compared with minimal intervention. It wasn't clearly better than other forms of exercise. If you have back pain, get it assessed first, and ask the instructor to adapt exercises for you.

### Does Pilates improve flexibility?

Pilates works through controlled ranges of motion, which can improve how freely you move over time. It isn't primarily a stretching class, though. If flexibility is your main goal, check how much of a class is spent on mobility work.

## Comparing Pilates

How it stacks up against other popular workouts.

### What's the difference between Pilates and yoga?

Both use the mat, breathing and body control. Pilates centers on core strength and precise movement, often with equipment. Yoga combines postures, breathing and, in many styles, meditation. Our [Pilates vs yoga](/fitness/pilates-vs-yoga/) comparison goes through the differences in detail.

### Is Pilates the same as barre?

No. Barre borrows from ballet, using small pulses, long holds and lots of repetitions, often at a ballet barre. Pilates is its own method focused on control and trunk stability, with far more research behind it. Our [barre vs Pilates](/fitness/barre-vs-pilates/) guide compares the two.

## Cost and Getting Started

What you'll pay and how often to go.

### How often should I do Pilates?

There's no single rule, and research trials often use about 2 sessions a week. Count Pilates toward the US guideline of muscle-strengthening work on at least 2 days a week, and keep doing aerobic exercise too.

### How much do Pilates classes cost?

It varies by city, studio and class type. Reformer classes usually cost more than mat classes, because each person needs a machine and class sizes are smaller. Use the [Pilates Class Cost Calculator](/pilates-class-cost-calculator/) to compare drop-in rates, class packs and memberships against how often you'll go.

### Can I do Pilates at home?

Yes. Mat Pilates needs little more than a mat and some floor space. Home reformers exist but cost much more and take up room. Our [Pilates apps](/fitness/pilates-apps/) comparison covers guided home classes for mat and reformer.
