# About Wellness Signal

Wellness Signal is a warm but rigorous publication covering wellness, fitness, sleep, recovery, aesthetics, workplace wellness, and wellness travel.

## Editorial Method

We distinguish market demand, consumer behavior, and product claims from evidence of health outcomes. When research is discussed, the relevant population, intervention, measure, and limitations should remain visible.
