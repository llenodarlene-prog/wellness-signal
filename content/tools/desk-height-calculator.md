A desk at the wrong height makes you reach, hunch or shrug for hours at a time. The fix starts with your own body, not a standard desk size. OSHA's computer workstation guidance says your keyboard should sit at about elbow height, with the top of your screen at or slightly below eye level.

This desk height calculator turns your measurements into the right heights for sitting or standing.

## How to Measure Yourself

You need a tape measure and a minute. Measure in the position you'll actually work in.

1. **Sit or stand as you work.** Sit with your feet flat on the floor or a footrest, or stand in your work shoes.
2. **Relax your shoulders.** Let your upper arms hang by your sides.
3. **Bend your elbows to about 90 degrees.** Keep your forearms level.
4. **Measure elbow height.** Go from the floor to the underside of your forearm.
5. **Measure eye height.** Go from the floor to your eyes while looking straight ahead.

## How the Calculator Works

| What | Where it should be (OSHA) |
|---|---|
| Keyboard home row | About elbow height |
| Desk or keyboard-tray surface | Elbow height minus your keyboard's height |
| Top line of the screen | At or slightly below eye level |
| Screen distance | 20 to 40 inches (50 to 100 cm) from your eyes |

OSHA's purchasing guide says keyboard height should adjust from 22 to 30 inches for seated work and from 36 to 46.5 inches for standing work. The calculator flags your result if it falls outside those ranges.

### A Worked Example

Say your seated elbow height is 27 inches and your keyboard is 1 inch tall at the home row. Your desk surface should be about 26 inches. If your eye height is 47 inches, the top of your screen should sit at or just below 47 inches.

## If Your Desk Doesn't Adjust

If your desk is fixed, OSHA suggests a few workarounds.

- **Desk too high:** raise your chair until your elbows reach keyboard height, then use a footrest so your feet stay supported.
- **Keyboard too high:** add a keyboard tray if the desk or chair can't be adjusted properly.
- **Monitor too high:** lower the monitor, or raise your chair and add a footrest.

OSHA notes that there's no single correct posture for everyone. Change positions through the day, and readjust if you switch between sitting and standing.

## FAQs

### What Is the Right Desk Height for My Height?

It depends on your elbow height rather than your total height. OSHA says your keyboard should sit at about elbow height. Measure from the floor to the underside of your forearm with your elbows bent about 90 degrees, then subtract your keyboard's height.

### How High Should My Monitor Be?

OSHA says the top line of the screen should be at or slightly below eye level, about 20 to 40 inches from your eyes. If you wear bifocals, OSHA suggests lowering the monitor so you don't tilt your head back.

### What Height Should a Standing Desk Be?

Set it so your keyboard is at about elbow height while you stand, with relaxed shoulders and elbows bent about 90 degrees. OSHA's purchasing guidance says standing keyboard height should adjust from 36 to 46.5 inches.
