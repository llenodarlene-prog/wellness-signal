A MET, or metabolic equivalent, compares an activity's energy cost with resting. One MET is your energy use sitting quietly, and an activity rated 4 METs uses about four times as much. This METs calculator turns a MET value and duration into MET-minutes, an intensity level and, if you add your weight, estimated calories.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Find the MET value for your activity in the Compendium of Physical Activities, the standard reference.
2. Enter the MET value and how many minutes you spent on it.
3. Optionally, enter your weight for a calorie estimate.
4. Read your MET-minutes, intensity and calories.

## How the Calculator Works

The 2024 Adult Compendium defines 1 MET as 3.5 mL of oxygen per kilogram per minute, or approximately 1.0 calorie per kilogram per hour.

| Step | Formula |
|---|---|
| MET-minutes | MET × minutes |
| Calories | MET × body weight (kg) × hours |

| Intensity | MET range |
|---|---|
| Light | Under 3 |
| Moderate | 3 to 5.9 |
| Vigorous | 6 or more |

### A Worked Example

A 154-pound person (69.9 kg) does 30 minutes of an activity rated 4 METs.

1. **MET-minutes:** 4 × 30 = **120 MET-minutes**
2. **Intensity:** 4 METs is **moderate**
3. **Calories:** 4 × 69.9 × 0.5 hours = **about 140 calories**

## Why MET-Minutes Matter

MET-minutes put different activities on one scale. Thirty minutes at 4 METs and twenty minutes at 6 METs both add up to 120 MET-minutes. ## Limitations

- **MET values are averages.** They describe a typical person doing a typical version of the activity.
- **Resting rates vary.** The 3.5 mL/kg/min standard doesn't fit everyone, especially older adults.
- **Calories are estimates.** Fitness, effort and technique change the real energy cost.

## FAQs

### What Is a MET?

A MET is a unit comparing an activity's energy cost with resting. The 2024 Compendium defines 1 MET as 3.5 mL of oxygen per kilogram per minute, about 1 calorie per kilogram per hour.

### How Do I Calculate Calories From METs?

Multiply the MET value by your weight in kilograms and the hours of activity. 4 METs for 30 minutes at 70 kg is about 140 calories.

### What Counts as Moderate Intensity?

Activities from 3 to 5.9 METs are moderate, and 6 METs or more is vigorous. Below 3 METs is light activity.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
