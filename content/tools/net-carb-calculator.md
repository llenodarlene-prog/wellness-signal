Net carbs are the carbohydrates left after you take away fiber, and sometimes sugar alcohols. This net carb calculator does that arithmetic from a nutrition label. There's no official definition of net carbs, and US and EU labels list carbohydrates differently, so the calculator asks which kind of label you have.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Choose your label type: US, or EU and UK.
2. Enter the carbohydrate figure. On a US label, also enter dietary fiber.
3. Enter sugar alcohols (polyols) if the label lists them, and choose whether to subtract them.
4. Read your net carbs per serving.

## How the Calculator Works

| Label | What "carbohydrate" includes | Net carbs |
|---|---|---|
| US | Total carbohydrate includes fiber and sugar alcohols | Total carbohydrate − fiber (− sugar alcohols, if you choose) |
| EU and UK | Carbohydrate includes polyols, but fiber is listed separately | Carbohydrate (− polyols, if you choose) |

Under US rules (21 CFR 101.9), total carbohydrate is calculated by difference, so it includes fiber and sugar alcohols. The EU's labeling regulation (1169/2011) defines carbohydrate as carbohydrate that's metabolized by humans, including polyols, and defines fiber separately.

### A Worked Example

A US label lists 30 g total carbohydrate, 10 g dietary fiber and 8 g sugar alcohols.

1. **Subtract fiber:** 30 − 10 = 20 g
2. **Subtract sugar alcohols:** 20 − 8 = **12 g net carbs**

If you don't subtract sugar alcohols, the result is 20 g.

## Why Sugar Alcohols Are a Judgment Call

Sugar alcohols aren't all alike. The FDA's labeling rules assign them calorie values from 0 calories per gram for erythritol up to 3.0 for hydrogenated starch hydrolysates, with others such as maltitol at 2.1 and sorbitol at 2.6. Some people subtract them all, some subtract none. The calculator lets you pick, and shows both figures in the breakdown.

## Limitations

- **Net carbs isn't a legal label term.** Brands that print "net carbs" may calculate it differently.
- **It's not a blood sugar prediction.** Foods with the same net carbs can affect blood sugar differently.
- **If you manage diabetes,** follow your care team's advice on carbohydrate counting.

## FAQs

### How Do You Calculate Net Carbs?

On a US label, subtract dietary fiber from total carbohydrate, and subtract sugar alcohols too if you choose. 30 g total minus 10 g fiber and 8 g sugar alcohols is 12 g net carbs.

### Do You Subtract Fiber on EU or UK Labels?

No. EU and UK labels already list fiber separately from carbohydrate, so subtracting it again would undercount. You may still choose to subtract polyols.

### Should I Subtract Sugar Alcohols From Net Carbs?

There's no official rule. Sugar alcohols have different calorie values, from 0 calories per gram for erythritol to 3.0 for some others. Choose a convention and use it consistently.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
