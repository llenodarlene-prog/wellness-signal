This fiber calculator shows how much dietary fiber adults are advised to eat each day. It uses the Adequate Intake (AI) values, which are based on 14 grams of fiber for every 1,000 calories. Choose your age group and sex for the standard figure, or enter your daily calories to scale it to your intake.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Choose your sex and age group.
2. Optionally, enter your usual daily calories.
3. Read your reference daily fiber in grams.

## How the Calculator Works

| Group | Adequate Intake (g per day) |
|---|---|
| Women 19 to 50 | 25 |
| Women 51 and over | 21 |
| Men 19 to 50 | 38 |
| Men 51 and over | 30 |

The AI is based on **14 g of fiber per 1,000 calories**. For your own calorie intake: fiber = 14 × calories ÷ 1,000.

### A Worked Example

A woman aged 35 has an AI of **25 g** a day. If she eats about 2,000 calories, the same basis gives 14 × 2,000 ÷ 1,000 = **28 g** a day.

## Most People Fall Short

A 2026 systematic review in *The American Journal of Clinical Nutrition* noted that most people in the US and Canada eat less fiber than the 14 g per 1,000 calories recommendation. Whole grains, beans, lentils, vegetables, fruit, nuts and seeds are all good sources.

If you track macros, the [Macro Calculator](/macro-calculator/) splits your calories into protein, carbs and fat. Fiber counts as part of total carbohydrate on US labels.

## Limitations

- **It's a reference amount, not a prescription.** Needs vary with health, diet and activity.
- **Some conditions call for different advice.** People with digestive disorders or kidney disease should ask a clinician.
- **Increase gradually.** A sudden jump in fiber can cause bloating and gas. Drink enough fluid as you add more.

## FAQs

### How Much Fiber Should I Eat a Day?

The Adequate Intake is 25 g for women and 38 g for men aged 19 to 50, and 21 g and 30 g for those 51 and over. These are based on 14 g of fiber per 1,000 calories.

### How Do I Calculate Fiber Needs From Calories?

Multiply your daily calories by 14 and divide by 1,000. At 2,000 calories a day, that's 28 g of fiber.

### Can You Eat Too Much Fiber?

Adding a lot of fiber quickly can cause bloating, gas and discomfort. Increase it gradually and drink enough fluid. If you have a digestive condition, ask a clinician what's right for you.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
