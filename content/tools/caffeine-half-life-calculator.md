Caffeine leaves your body gradually. Its half-life is the time it takes for half of it to be cleared. This caffeine half-life calculator estimates how much is left after a given number of hours. Research reviews put the half-life at roughly 3 to 7 hours in adults, and it varies a lot from person to person, so you can adjust it.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter your caffeine dose in milligrams. The [Caffeine Intake Calculator](/caffeine-intake-calculator/) can estimate it.
2. Enter how many hours ago you had it.
3. Adjust the half-life if you like. The default is 5 hours.
4. Read how much is left, plus a timeline.

## How the Calculator Works

Caffeine remaining = dose × 0.5^(hours ÷ half-life)

| Half-lives passed | Share remaining |
|---|---|
| 1 | 50% |
| 2 | 25% |
| 3 | 12.5% |

### A Worked Example

You drink 200 mg of caffeine. Eight hours later, with a 5-hour half-life:

1. **Half-lives passed:** 8 ÷ 5 = 1.6
2. **Remaining:** 200 × 0.5^1.6 = 200 × 0.33 = **about 66 mg**

At 10 hours, two full half-lives, 50 mg would remain.

## Why Half-Life Varies

A 2026 review in *Metabolites* describes caffeine's plasma half-life as approximately 3 to 7 hours. A 2026 review in *Frontiers in Physiology* puts it at 4 to 6 hours in many adults. Differences in the liver enzyme CYP1A2 are one reason people clear caffeine at different speeds. Try 3 and 7 hours in the calculator to see the range.

## Limitations

- **It's a simple model.** Real clearance depends on genetics, pregnancy, medicines, liver health and more.
- **"Left in your body" isn't the same as "no effect."** The calculator can't tell you when caffeine stops affecting your sleep.
- **It assumes one dose.** For several drinks, add up what's left from each.

## FAQs

### What Is the Half-Life of Caffeine?

Reviews put it at roughly 3 to 7 hours in adults, with 4 to 6 hours common. It varies widely between people.

### How Long Does Caffeine Stay in Your System?

It depends on your half-life. With a 5-hour half-life, a quarter of the caffeine is still there after 10 hours and an eighth after 15 hours.

### How Much Caffeine Is Left After 8 Hours?

With a 5-hour half-life, about a third remains. From a 200 mg dose, that's about 66 mg.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
