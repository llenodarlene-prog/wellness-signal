Body mass index, or BMI, compares your weight with your height. It's a quick screening number that doctors use to flag possible weight-related health risks. This BMI calculator uses the standard formula and the CDC's categories for adults aged 20 and older.

Enter your height and weight in either unit. You'll see your BMI, your category and the weight range that counts as healthy for your height.

## How the Calculator Works

BMI is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in meters, squared.

| CDC category, adults 20+ | BMI |
|---|---|
| Underweight | Less than 18.5 |
| Healthy weight | 18.5 to less than 25 |
| Overweight | 25 to less than 30 |
| Obesity, class 1 | 30 to less than 35 |
| Obesity, class 2 | 35 to less than 40 |
| Obesity, class 3 (severe) | 40 or more |

The healthy weight range for your height runs from a BMI of 18.5 up to just under 25.

### A Worked Example

Take someone who is 5 feet 6 inches tall (66 inches, or 1.68 meters) and weighs 160 pounds (72.6 kg).

1. **Height squared:** 1.676 × 1.676 = 2.81
2. **BMI:** 72.6 ÷ 2.81 = 25.8
3. **Category:** overweight, just above the healthy range
4. **Healthy range for that height:** about 115 to 154 pounds

## What BMI Can't Tell You

The CDC calls BMI a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It has real limits.

- **It doesn't measure body fat.** Very muscular people can have a high BMI with little body fat.
- **It ignores where fat sits.** Fat around the waist carries more risk than fat elsewhere, which BMI can't see.
- **It isn't for children.** Kids and teens use BMI-for-age percentiles, because their bodies change as they grow.

## Using Your Result

A single BMI reading is a starting point for a conversation, not a verdict. Blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, fitness and family history all matter too. If your BMI is outside the healthy range, a clinician can help you work out whether it matters for you.

## FAQs

### What Is a Healthy BMI for Adults?

The CDC classes a BMI of 18.5 to less than 25 as healthy weight for adults aged 20 and older. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25 to less than 30 is overweight and 30 or more is obesity. These are screening ranges, not diagnoses.

### Is BMI Accurate for Muscular People?

Not always. BMI uses only height and weight, so it can't tell muscle from fat. Athletes and very muscular people may land in the overweight range with little body fat. A waist measurement or body composition test gives more context.

### Can I Use This BMI Calculator for My Child?

No. The CDC uses BMI-for-age percentiles for children and teens aged 2 to 19, because healthy body fat changes with age and sex. Use a pediatric BMI tool or ask your child's doctor.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
