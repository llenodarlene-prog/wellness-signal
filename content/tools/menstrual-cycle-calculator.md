This menstrual cycle calculator estimates when your next three periods will start. It adds your usual cycle length to the first day of your last period, and shows a window around each date to allow for natural variation. It's simple calendar math. It doesn't predict ovulation or fertile days, and it isn't birth control.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter the first day of your last period, the day bleeding started.
2. Enter your usual cycle length: the days from the first day of one period to the first day of the next.
3. Enter how many days your cycle usually varies.
4. Read your estimated next three start dates.

## How the Calculator Works

| Step | Formula |
|---|---|
| Next period | First day of last period + cycle length |
| Following periods | Add one more cycle length each time |
| Window | Predicted date ± how many days your cycle varies |

### A Worked Example

Say your last period started on November 1, your cycle is 28 days and it varies by about 2 days.

1. **Next period:** November 1 + 28 days = **November 29**, between November 27 and December 1
2. **The one after:** November 29 + 28 days = **December 27**
3. **Third:** December 27 + 28 days = **January 24**

## What's a Typical Cycle Length?

The International Federation of Gynecology and Obstetrics (FIGO) describes a regular menstrual cycle as one that comes every 24 to 38 days. Its 2018 definitions also describe a normal period as lasting up to 8 days. Cycles often vary a little month to month. If yours is regularly outside that range, or changes suddenly, mention it to a clinician.

## Limitations

- **It's an estimate.** Stress, illness, travel, medicines, postpartum changes and perimenopause can all shift your cycle.
- **It doesn't show fertile days or ovulation.** Don't use it for birth control or to plan pregnancy.
- **Irregular cycles make predictions less useful.** If your cycle varies a lot, the windows will be wide.

## FAQs

### How Do I Calculate When My Next Period Will Start?

Add your usual cycle length to the first day of your last period. If your last period started November 1 and your cycle is 28 days, your next period would start around November 29.

### What Is a Normal Menstrual Cycle Length?

FIGO describes regular cycles as 24 to 38 days long. Many people have cycles that vary by a few days from month to month.

### Can I Use This to Avoid Pregnancy?

No. Calendar estimates can't reliably predict fertile days. Talk to a clinician or pharmacist about birth control options.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
