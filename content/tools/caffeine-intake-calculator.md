This caffeine intake calculator adds up the caffeine in your drinks for the day. Caffeine varies a lot between brands and brews, so instead of one exact number it gives a range based on the FDA's typical amounts. Add exact figures from labels where you have them, and compare your total with the 400 mg a day the FDA cites for most adults.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter how many 12-fluid-ounce servings of each drink you've had. Half servings are fine.
2. For anything with a stated amount on the label, such as an espresso or pre-workout, enter the milligrams under Other.
3. Read your estimated range and how it compares with 400 mg.

## How the Calculator Works

Total caffeine = the sum of servings × the FDA's typical caffeine per 12 fl oz, plus any label amounts.

| Drink, 12 fl oz | FDA typical caffeine |
|---|---|
| Brewed coffee (regular, non-specialty) | 113 to 247 mg |
| Black tea | 71 mg |
| Green tea | 37 mg |
| Caffeinated soft drink | 23 to 83 mg |
| Energy drink | 41 to 246 mg |

### A Worked Example

Two 12-ounce cups of brewed coffee and one 12-ounce black tea:

1. **Coffee:** 2 × 113 to 2 × 247 = 226 to 494 mg
2. **Tea:** 1 × 71 = 71 mg
3. **Total:** **297 to 565 mg**

The upper end is above 400 mg, which shows how much brewed coffee can vary.

## What the 400 mg Figure Means

The FDA says that for most adults, 400 mg a day, about two to three 12-ounce cups of coffee, is an amount not generally associated with negative effects. It's a population reference, not a personal safe limit. The FDA notes that how much is too much varies with body weight, medicines, health conditions and sensitivity. It recommends talking to a health care provider if you're pregnant, trying to become pregnant or breastfeeding, or have concerns about a condition or medicine.

## Limitations

- **Amounts vary widely.** Coffee strength, brewing method and cup size all change the real figure.
- **Cup sizes differ.** The table uses 12 fl oz. A large coffee-shop cup may hold 16 or 20 fl oz.
- **Hidden sources count too.** Chocolate, some pain relievers and supplements contain caffeine.

## FAQs

### How Much Caffeine Is in a Cup of Coffee?

The FDA lists 113 to 247 mg for a 12-fluid-ounce cup of regular brewed coffee. The wide range reflects differences in beans, brewing and strength.

### How Much Caffeine Is Too Much in a Day?

The FDA cites 400 mg a day for most adults as an amount not generally associated with negative effects. The FDA notes the right amount varies by person, and recommends talking to a health care provider if you're pregnant, trying to become pregnant or breastfeeding.

### Does Decaf Coffee Have Caffeine?

Yes, a little. The FDA says decaf coffee typically has 2 to 15 mg in an 8-fluid-ounce cup.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
