This walking time calculator tells you how long a walk will take from its distance and your speed. Enter miles or kilometers, set your pace and add any break time. It also shows your pace per mile and per kilometer, and how long the same walk would take at other common speeds.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter the distance and choose miles or kilometers.
2. Set your walking speed in miles per hour. The CDC counts 2.5 mph or faster as brisk walking.
3. Add break time if you'll stop along the way.
4. Read your total time, moving time and pace.

## How the Calculator Works

| Step | Formula |
|---|---|
| Moving time | Distance ÷ speed |
| Total time | Moving time + breaks |
| Pace | 60 ÷ speed in mph = minutes per mile |
| Kilometers to miles | Kilometers ÷ 1.609344 |

### A Worked Example

A 3-mile walk at 3 mph:

1. **Moving time:** 3 ÷ 3 = 1 hour, or **1:00:00**
2. **Pace:** 60 ÷ 3 = **20 minutes per mile**

Add 15 minutes of breaks and the total becomes 1 hour 15 minutes.

## What Counts as Brisk Walking?

The CDC lists walking briskly at 2.5 miles per hour or faster as a moderate-intensity activity. At that speed, a mile takes 24 minutes. ## Limitations

- **Assumes a steady pace on flat ground.** Hills, stairs and rough trails slow you down.
- **Real walks have stops.** Crossings, traffic lights and crowds add time. Add them as breaks.
- **Speed estimates vary.** Phone and watch speeds can be off, especially in cities with tall buildings.

## FAQs

### How Long Does It Take to Walk a Mile?

At 3 mph, a mile takes 20 minutes. At the CDC's brisk-walking threshold of 2.5 mph, it takes 24 minutes. At 3.5 mph, it takes about 17 minutes.

### How Long Does It Take to Walk 5 Kilometers?

5 km is about 3.1 miles. At 3 mph, that takes about 1 hour 2 minutes of walking.

### What Is a Normal Walking Speed?

Speeds vary with age, fitness and terrain. The CDC counts 2.5 mph or faster as brisk walking, which is moderate-intensity exercise.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
