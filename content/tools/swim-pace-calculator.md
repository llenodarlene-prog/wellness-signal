Swimmers measure pace as time per 100 meters or per 100 yards. This swim pace calculator converts a distance and time into both, counts the lengths in your pool and projects your time for common race distances at the same pace. It uses the exact conversion of 1 yard to 0.9144 meters.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter the distance you swam and choose meters or yards.
2. Enter your time as mm:ss or h:mm:ss.
3. Choose your pool length: 25 yards, 25 meters or 50 meters.
4. Read your pace per 100 m and 100 yd, lengths and projections.

## How the Calculator Works

| Step | Formula |
|---|---|
| Pace per 100 | Time ÷ distance × 100 |
| Yards to meters | Yards × 0.9144 |
| Pool lengths | Distance ÷ pool length |

### A Worked Example

You swim 1,000 yards in 20:00 in a 25-yard pool.

1. **Per 100 yd:** 1,200 seconds ÷ 1,000 × 100 = 120 seconds, or **2:00**
2. **Per 100 m:** 1,000 yd = 914.4 m, so 1,200 ÷ 914.4 × 100 = 131 seconds, or **2:11**
3. **Lengths:** 1,000 ÷ 25 = **40 lengths**

## Short Course vs Long Course

A 25-yard or 25-meter pool is a short course, and a 50-meter pool is a long course. A short-course swim has twice as many turns as the same distance in a 50-meter pool, so compare paces from the same pool length when you can.

## Limitations

- **Assumes an even pace.** Real swims include turns, rests and changes of stroke.
- **Projections are not predictions.** Holding the same pace over a longer distance is harder.
- **Pool lengths affect times.** Short-course and long-course paces aren't directly comparable.

## FAQs

### How Do I Calculate My Swim Pace per 100?

Divide your time by the distance and multiply by 100. 1,000 yards in 20 minutes is 2:00 per 100 yards.

### How Do I Convert Yards to Meters for Swimming?

Multiply yards by 0.9144. 1,000 yards is 914.4 meters. Pace per 100 m is slower on the clock than pace per 100 yd, because 100 m is longer.

### How Many Lengths Is a Mile in a 25-Yard Pool?

A mile is 1,760 yards, so it's 70.4 lengths of a 25-yard pool.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
