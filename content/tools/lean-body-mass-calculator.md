Lean body mass is everything in your body that isn't fat: muscle, bone, organs, skin and water. This lean body mass calculator splits your weight into lean mass and fat mass from a body fat percentage you already have. It's simple arithmetic, so the result is only as good as the body fat number you enter.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter your body weight in pounds or kilograms.
2. Enter your body fat percentage from a smart scale, DEXA scan or the [Navy Body Fat Calculator](/navy-body-fat-calculator/).
3. Read your lean body mass and fat mass in both units.

## How the Calculator Works

| Step | Formula |
|---|---|
| Fat mass | Body weight × body fat % ÷ 100 |
| Lean body mass | Body weight − fat mass |

### A Worked Example

Take someone who weighs 180 pounds with 25% body fat.

1. **Fat mass:** 180 × 25 ÷ 100 = **45 lb**
2. **Lean body mass:** 180 − 45 = **135 lb**, or about 61.2 kg

## Lean Mass Is Not the Same as Muscle

A common mistake is to treat lean body mass as muscle mass. Muscle is only part of it. Bone, organs, connective tissue and body water all count as lean mass, so your muscle mass is always lower than your lean mass.

## Where Your Body Fat Number Comes From Matters

Your result depends entirely on the body fat percentage you enter. A 2021 study comparing three smart scales with DEXA scans found fat mass errors of 2.2 to 4.4 kg. Our guide to the [best body composition scales](/fitness/best-body-composition-scales/) explains why scale readings work best as trends. If you use one method, keep using it so changes over time are comparable.

Lean mass is also useful for setting protein targets, which the [Protein Calculator](/protein-calculator/) can help with.

## Limitations

- **Garbage in, garbage out.** An inaccurate body fat percentage gives an inaccurate lean mass.
- **Hydration shifts readings.** Water counts as lean mass, so it can change from day to day.
- **It's not a health verdict.** Talk to a clinician about what your body composition means for you.

## FAQs

### How Do I Calculate Lean Body Mass?

Multiply your weight by your body fat percentage and divide by 100 to get fat mass, then subtract that from your weight. At 180 pounds and 25% body fat, that's 45 pounds of fat and 135 pounds of lean mass.

### Is Lean Body Mass the Same as Muscle Mass?

No. Lean body mass includes muscle plus bone, organs, connective tissue and water. Muscle mass is only part of it.

### How Can I Find My Body Fat Percentage?

Options include a smart scale, a DEXA scan or a tape-measure method like the US Navy equations. DEXA is the most precise of these. Scales and tape methods are estimates, but they're useful for tracking change over time.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
