Rucking means walking with a weighted pack. This rucking calorie calculator estimates the energy you burn using the Pandolf equation, a load-carriage model developed for military research in 1977. It accounts for your body weight, pack weight, speed, uphill grade and surface. It also shows how many extra calories the pack adds compared with the same walk without it.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter your body weight and pack weight in pounds or kilograms.
2. Enter your walking speed and any uphill grade.
3. Choose the surface and how long you walked.
4. Read your estimated calories and the extra from the pack.

## How the Calculator Works

The Pandolf equation estimates metabolic rate in watts:

M = 1.5W + 2.0(W + L)(L ÷ W)² + η(W + L)(1.5V² + 0.35VG)

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| W | Body mass, kg |
| L | Load mass, kg |
| V | Speed, meters per second |
| G | Grade, % |
| η | Terrain factor: 1.0 for pavement or treadmill; 1.08 for grass, as measured in a 2021 field study |

Calories per hour = watts × 3,600 ÷ 4,184.

### A Worked Example

A 180-pound person (81.6 kg) carries a 30-pound pack (13.6 kg) at 3 mph (1.34 m/s) on flat pavement for an hour.

1. **Standing cost:** 1.5 × 81.6 = 122.5 W
2. **Load term:** 2.0 × 95.3 × (13.6 ÷ 81.6)² = 5.3 W
3. **Walking term:** 1.0 × 95.3 × (1.5 × 1.34²) = 257.0 W
4. **Total:** 384.7 W × 3,600 ÷ 4,184 = **about 331 calories per hour**

The same walk without the pack is about 295 calories, so the pack adds about 36. Add a 5% grade and the total rises to about 523 calories per hour.

## How Accurate Is It?

A 2021 study compared walking equations with measured energy use during outdoor walks. For loaded walking, the Pandolf equation overpredicted by about 13%. Treat the result as an upper-leaning estimate. The equation also isn't designed for downhill walking.

## Limitations

- **It's an estimate.** Fitness, walking style and pack fit all change real energy use.
- **Not for downhill walking.** The equation was built for level and uphill walking.
- **Build up gradually.** Start with a light pack and short distances. Check with a clinician if you have back, joint or heart problems.

## FAQs

### How Many Calories Does Rucking Burn?

It depends on your weight, pack, speed and terrain. A 180-pound person carrying 30 pounds at 3 mph on flat ground burns about 331 calories an hour by the Pandolf equation, about 36 more than walking without the pack.

### Does Rucking Burn More Calories Than Walking?

Yes. Carrying extra weight increases energy use, and walking uphill increases it more. In the example above, a 5% grade raises the estimate from 331 to about 523 calories an hour.

### How Heavy Should My Rucking Pack Be?

Start light and build up over weeks. The calculator flags any pack over 60% of your body weight as likely a data entry error. If you have health concerns, ask a clinician before adding load.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
