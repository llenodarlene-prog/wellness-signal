This intermittent fasting calculator works out when your fast ends and when your eating window runs. Enter the time you finished your last meal and choose a schedule such as 16:8, where you fast for 16 hours and eat within 8. It handles fasts that run past midnight. It's a timing tool only, not a recommendation to fast.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter the time your fast starts, when you finish your last meal.
2. Choose a schedule: 12:12, 14:10, 16:8, 18:6 or custom.
3. Read when your fast ends and your eating window.

## How the Calculator Works

| Step | Formula |
|---|---|
| Fast ends | Start time + fasting hours |
| Eating window | 24 − fasting hours, starting when the fast ends |

### A Worked Example

You finish dinner at 8:00 PM and follow a 16:8 schedule.

1. **Fast ends:** 8:00 PM + 16 hours = **12:00 PM the next day**
2. **Eating window:** 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM, **8 hours**

## Who Shouldn't Try Intermittent Fasting

Healthdirect, the Australian government's health information service, says intermittent fasting is not recommended for:

- Children and teenagers
- People with a current or past eating disorder
- People who are pregnant or breastfeeding
- People with certain medical conditions

It also advises talking to your doctor first if you're having cancer treatment or taking diabetes medicines.

## Limitations

- **It's a clock, not advice.** The calculator makes no claims about health effects or weight loss.
- **Your schedule can vary.** Work, travel and time zones change real eating times.
- **Longer fasts need care.** Talk to a clinician before fasting for more than a day.

## FAQs

### What Does 16:8 Intermittent Fasting Mean?

You fast for 16 hours and eat within an 8-hour window each day. If you stop eating at 8:00 PM, you'd eat again at noon the next day and stop by 8:00 PM.

### When Does a 16-Hour Fast End?

Add 16 hours to when you finished eating. A fast starting at 7:30 PM ends at 11:30 AM the next day.

### Is Intermittent Fasting Safe for Everyone?

No. Healthdirect says it isn't recommended for children and teenagers, people with a current or past eating disorder, or during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Talk to a doctor first if you take diabetes medicines.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
