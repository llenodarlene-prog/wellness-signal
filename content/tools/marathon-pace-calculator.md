A marathon is 42.195 kilometers, or about 26.2 miles. This marathon pace calculator turns your goal finish time into the pace you need per mile and per kilometer. It also gives even-pace split times so you can check your progress on race day.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter your goal time as hours, minutes and seconds, such as 4:00:00.
2. Read your pace per mile and per kilometer.
3. Use the split table to see where you should be at each 5K and at halfway.

## How the Calculator Works

| Step | Formula |
|---|---|
| Pace per mile | Finish time ÷ 26.219 miles |
| Pace per kilometer | Finish time ÷ 42.195 km |
| Split at any point | Finish time × (distance so far ÷ 42.195 km) |

### A Worked Example

For a 4-hour marathon, which is 14,400 seconds:

1. **Per mile:** 14,400 ÷ 26.219 = 549 seconds, or **9:09 per mile**
2. **Per kilometer:** 14,400 ÷ 42.195 = 341 seconds, or **5:41 per km**
3. **Halfway:** exactly **2:00:00**

## Using Your Splits

Even pacing is a planning baseline, not a rule. Hills, heat and crowded starts all shift real splits, and GPS watches often read a little long over a full marathon. For a shorter race, the [Half Marathon Pace Calculator](/half-marathon-pace-calculator/) works the same way, and the [Running Pace Calculator](/running-pace-calculator/) handles any distance.

## FAQs

### What Pace Do I Need for a 4-Hour Marathon?

About 9:09 per mile, or 5:41 per kilometer, held for the whole race. Your halfway split would be 2:00:00.

### How Long Is a Marathon Exactly?

World Athletics sets the marathon at 42.195 kilometers, which is about 26.219 miles.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
