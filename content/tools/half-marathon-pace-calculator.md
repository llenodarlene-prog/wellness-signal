A half marathon is 21.0975 kilometers, or about 13.1 miles: exactly half a marathon. This half marathon pace calculator turns your goal time into the pace you need per mile and per kilometer, with even-pace split times along the course.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter your goal time, such as 2:00:00.
2. Read your pace per mile and per kilometer.
3. Check the split table for 5K, 10K, 15K and 20K.

## How the Calculator Works

| Step | Formula |
|---|---|
| Pace per mile | Finish time ÷ 13.109 miles |
| Pace per kilometer | Finish time ÷ 21.0975 km |
| Split at any point | Finish time × (distance so far ÷ 21.0975 km) |

### A Worked Example

For a 2-hour half marathon, which is 7,200 seconds:

1. **Per mile:** 7,200 ÷ 13.109 = 549 seconds, or **9:09 per mile**
2. **Per kilometer:** 7,200 ÷ 21.0975 = 341 seconds, or **5:41 per km**
3. **10K split:** 7,200 × (10 ÷ 21.0975) = 3,413 seconds, or **56:53**

## Using Your Splits

Treat even splits as a baseline. Courses, weather and race-day nerves change real pacing. Moving up to the full distance? The [Marathon Pace Calculator](/marathon-pace-calculator/) gives splits to 40K, and the [Running Pace Calculator](/running-pace-calculator/) covers any distance.

## FAQs

### What Pace Do I Need for a 2-Hour Half Marathon?

About 9:09 per mile, or 5:41 per kilometer, held the whole way. You'd pass 10K in about 56:53.

### How Long Is a Half Marathon?

21.0975 kilometers, or about 13.1 miles, which is exactly half the 42.195-kilometer marathon.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
