Percentage change puts weight change in context: 10 pounds means something different at 150 pounds than at 300. This weight loss percentage calculator shows how much your weight has changed as a share of where you started. It also handles a gain correctly.

## How to Use This Calculator

1. Enter your starting weight.
2. Enter your current weight in the same unit.
3. Read the percentage change and the pounds or kilograms lost or gained.

## How the Calculator Works

Percent change = (current weight − starting weight) ÷ starting weight × 100

A negative result is a loss and a positive result is a gain.

### A Worked Example

Say you started at 200 pounds and now weigh 186.

1. **Difference:** 186 − 200 = −14 pounds
2. **Percent change:** −14 ÷ 200 × 100 = **−7%**, a 7% loss

## Putting the Number in Context

The CDC notes that people who lose weight gradually, at about 1 to 2 pounds a week, are more likely to keep it off. If you're planning intake, the [Calorie Deficit Calculator](/calorie-deficit-calculator/) shows a daily target from your maintenance calories, and the [BMI Calculator](/bmi-calculator/) gives a separate screening measure.

## Limitations

- **Daily weight fluctuates.** Fluid, meals and timing can move the scale, so compare readings taken in similar conditions.
- **The scale doesn't show composition.** It can't separate fat, muscle and water.
- **Weight isn't the whole picture.** Talk to a clinician about what change is healthy for you.

## FAQs

### How Do I Calculate Weight Loss Percentage?

Subtract your current weight from your starting weight, divide by your starting weight, then multiply by 100. Going from 200 to 186 pounds is a 14-pound loss, or 7%.

### Does It Matter Which Unit I Use?

No, as long as both weights use the same unit. The percentage is the same in pounds or kilograms.

### Is My Information Stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, doesn't save your entries and doesn't send them anywhere. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.
