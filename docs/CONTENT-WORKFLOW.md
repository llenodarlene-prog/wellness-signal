# Content Workflow

1. Select an approved route from `data/content-plan.json`.
2. Complete live Ahrefs research and map five ranking competitors. Use Ubersuggest as a secondary cross-check.
3. Verify primary sources and record dates, definitions, study or survey populations, samples, measures, and limitations.
4. Separate consumer demand, market size, and product claims from evidence of health outcomes.
5. Move the research record from `scaffolded` to `verified`.
6. Draft with the approved keyword, 2 to 3 natural internal links, and at least 3 useful contextual external citations.
7. Run factual QA, evidence QA, content QA, SEO checks, and the AI-language check.
8. Record fact-check and QA sign-offs with UTC dates.
9. Change `draft: true` to `draft: false` only after every gate passes.
10. Open a feature pull request into `staging`, review the noindex deployment, then promote `staging` to `main`.
