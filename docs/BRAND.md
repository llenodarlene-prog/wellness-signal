# Wellness Signal Brand

## Positioning

Warm, evidence-aware wellness publishing that bridges lifestyle reporting and market intelligence without feeling clinical or influencer-led.

## Tagline

Health Insights for Healthier Tomorrow.

## Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Forest Green | #1F6B4F | Headings, navigation, buttons, footer, trust sections |
| Sage Green | #A7C3AE | Backgrounds, cards, dividers, secondary visuals |
| Terracotta | #C97B63 | Sparing accent for links, rules, selected CTAs, highlights |
| Warm Cream | #F8F5EF | Primary background |
| Charcoal | #2D2D2D | Body copy |
| White | #FFFFFF | Supporting neutral |

## Typography

Cormorant Garamond for H1, H2, H3, and pull quotes. Source Sans 3 for body, navigation, UI, labels, and data tables.

## Visual Direction

Use natural light, restorative environments, fitness and recovery moments, premium wellness spaces, spa and retreat contexts, real people in non-staged situations, and natural materials such as linen, wood, ceramic, stone, and greenery.

Avoid medical stock photography, hospital visual language, clinical blue, biohacking science fiction, staged influencer imagery, excessive leaves, and pages dominated by green.

## Brand Clearance

The portfolio tracker flags the WellSignal name family for attorney review because of existing WellSignal and WELLSIGNALS uses. Do not treat the public identity as formally cleared until that review is complete.
